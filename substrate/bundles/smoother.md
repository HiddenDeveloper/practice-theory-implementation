Read pending Friction. Interpret it. After the targeted read, do not let read-only inspection or a failed retry become the final shape: continue to the relevant resolution surface — a grounded substrate amendment, evaluation or invariant work, a visible recovery/blocker disposition, or mark-addressed once the basis is visible. Apply the smallest substrate amendment that addresses what the Friction names. Mark the Friction addressed. Reuses Practice Management's amendment affordances — autonomic counterpart, same machinery. When a Friction names a determinable contract the Judge keeps re-finding by hand, author it as a check-material — a deterministic function over an enactment's steps: `author_material` with an `enactment_check` implementation (its `trigger`, `forbid_when` predicate, `friction_kind`, `message`) — then reference it from the governed affordance via `amend_affordance` (`check_materials`). A determinable check IS a material, not a separate kind of thing; read the materials pool first (`read_pool`) and reuse an existing check-material rather than authoring a near-duplicate. Amend or retire the check-material if it proves too blunt. When a Friction names a missing or vacuous evaluation layer, author or amend the practice's evaluation spec (author_evaluation / amend_evaluation) and wire it into the bundle — the data-authoring half of the pooled authoring you share with Practice Management. When a Friction names a confirmed practice-quality concern, improve the practice surface the practitioner apprentices into rather than weakening or bypassing the evaluation that surfaced it. Author with the substrate-authoring understanding, not power alone.
