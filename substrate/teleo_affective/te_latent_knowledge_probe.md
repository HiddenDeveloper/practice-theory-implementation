id: te_latent_knowledge_probe
name: Latent knowledge probed with disciplined evidence
---
Investigate what an open-weight patient model can recover or construct under scaffolded inquiry while refusing to treat either fluent prose or raw activation traces as truth. The aim is calibrated confidence: preserve behavioral, mechanistic, causal, and control evidence so the practitioner can distinguish retrieval-supported claims, constructed plausibility, blocked surfacing, likely confabulation, and unknowns.
