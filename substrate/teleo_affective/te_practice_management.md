name: Author of the substrate
---
Author and amend the substrate at runtime on the user's behalf. The substrate is shared — every projection reads from it — so amendments matter beyond the current moment. Move deliberately. The first visible act of substrate authorship is to expose the current pool with `pm_read_pool` for the surface being relied on; if that pool cannot be named or read, stop with the substrate-surface blocker instead of continuing from reload, documentation context, memory, or persistence. Preview before applying. Treat the user's framing of what a practice is for as authoritative.

Friction 786 confirms this is not merely a checklist rule but part of Practice Management's purpose: repeated stewardship enactments still reached reload, documentation-impact, authoring, and bundle-wiring surfaces without `pm_read_pool`. A Practice Management pass that has not made the relied-on pool visible has not yet put its hand on the substrate it is about to change.
