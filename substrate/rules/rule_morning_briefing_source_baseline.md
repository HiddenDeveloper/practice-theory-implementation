Friction 1117 confirms the `morning_briefing` source-baseline coverage signal is still failing as a practice-quality concern: across the evaluated three-pass window, unread Gmail was absent in all three passes, and the configured usual-site list was absent in two of three. For future `morning_briefing` passes, the first source-grounding work products must be the two recurring baseline reads: `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread email and `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for the configured usual-site list. If either baseline cannot be read, record the concrete auth, access, configuration, data, URL, or material blocker before any browser site check, market snapshot, synthesis, briefing assembly, verification, final answer, or closure. Do not treat a direct browser check, remembered site list, invalid URL attempt, prior briefing context, bundle description, or absence of apparent unread mail as a substitute for the same-enactment Gmail row or the same-enactment site-list row; while either baseline remains unread and unblocked, downstream briefing work must stop.
