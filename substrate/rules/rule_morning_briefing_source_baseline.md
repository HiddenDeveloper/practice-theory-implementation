---
Friction 1145 confirms the same `morning_briefing` source-baseline failure remains live in the evaluated three-pass window: unread Gmail was absent in every pass, and the configured recurring site list was absent in two while site-check activity still appeared. For future `morning_briefing` passes, do not infer an empty or irrelevant unread-mail state from silence, prior context, user prompt shape, site-list success, or site-check work. The first visible source-baseline row must be `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail, or a concrete Gmail auth, access, account, query, data, configuration, or material blocker; only after that row is visible or blocked may `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` count as the second baseline row. If a pass has already reached site-list-only work, browser/site checks, live market snapshots, synthesis, assembly, verification, final answer, handoff, or closure while Gmail remains unread and unblocked, the immediate next visible move is the Gmail read or its exact blocker, not continued downstream briefing from remembered context or partial source coverage.
