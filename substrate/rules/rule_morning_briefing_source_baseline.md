---
Friction 1135 confirms the `morning_briefing` source-baseline objective is still failing despite the bundle description naming the expected order: in the evaluated three-pass window, every pass omitted unread Gmail via `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads`, and two passes also reached site-check activity without the configured recurring-site-list read via `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites`. For future `morning_briefing` passes, the first visible source-baseline work products must be both recurring reads: unread Gmail through `gmail_user_search_threads` and the configured usual-site list through `read_morning_briefing_sites`, or a concrete auth, access, configuration, data, or material blocker for each missing source. Do not continue into `capture_morning_site_check`, browser site checks, live market reads, synthesis, assembly, verification, or closure from remembered context, prompt text, prior morning context, or individual site checks while either baseline source remains unread and unblocked.

Friction 1138 confirms the remaining failure is not missing evaluation but source-baseline drift before briefing work: across the evaluated three-enactment window, unread Gmail was absent in all three direct step reads, and two passes also lacked the configured recurring-site-list row while proceeding to browser site checks. Treat the first browser/site-check, source interpretation, live snapshot, synthesis, handoff, final answer, verification, or closure as interrupted unless the same enactment has already produced the ordered two-row baseline: `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread Gmail first, then `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for configured recurring sites, or a concrete blocker for the exact missing source. A site-list read without Gmail, an individual browser check, an invalid-URL result, remembered morning context, or bundle prose cannot carry the briefing forward while either baseline row remains absent and unblocked.

Friction 1141 confirms the same `reads_morning_sources_before_assembling` coverage failure remains live in the evaluated `morning_briefing` window: unread Gmail was absent in all three passes, and two of three also lacked the configured recurring-site-list read, with no concrete Gmail or site-list blocker visible. For future morning briefings, the opening source-baseline ledger is the first work product: `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail first, then `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for configured recurring sites, unless the exact missing row has a concrete auth, access, account, configuration, data, query, or material blocker. If any downstream briefing surface, including site-list-only work, browser/site checks, live market snapshots, source interpretation, assembly, final answer, verification, handoff, or closure, appears while either baseline row is absent and unblocked, the pass is interrupted and the only valid continuation is the missing source read or its blocker.
