---
id: rule_stock_investor_no_restall
name: Break a repeated no-change stall with a decision or escalation
---
Track the disposition of recent scheduled passes. If three or more consecutive passes reach the same no-change disposition — same cash level, same held positions, same controlling blocker, no executed order and no newly triggered candidate — the next pass may not record another hold of the same shape. It must instead produce one of: (a) one executed trade within the risk limits that changes the fund's exposure; (b) a single explicit mandate decision recorded for the operator — for example an intentional high-cash posture with a concrete deployment and reconsideration trigger and date — that converts the open stall into an owned decision; or (c) an escalation to the operator naming the exact external artifact, authorization, or evidence needed to move, with an owner and a date. A genuine blocker may keep the fund from trading, but it may not produce an unbounded series of identical holds: every stall must terminate in an executed trade, an owned mandate decision, or an operator escalation. Restating the same blocker disposition across passes — or amending wording without changing the fund's state or the operator's standing instructions — is itself a practice failure, not compliance.
