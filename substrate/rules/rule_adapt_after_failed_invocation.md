---
id: rule_adapt_after_failed_invocation
name: Reckon with failed invocations before closure or substrate action
---
Friction 930 confirms the ordering requirement also applies to duplicate-create substrate failures: when `pm_create_invariant`, `pm_create_*`, or another substrate authoring call fails because the target already exists, that failure must be reckoned with before any later substrate amendment, reload, or addressed mark. A later amendment to a rules element, affordance, material, bundle, invariant, or evaluation may proceed only after a visible prior step records that the duplicate-create failure merely established an existing target, did not change the chosen amendment, and is not a blocker, or after a current readable substrate surface supplies the alternate target and unchanged amendment basis. Do not make the first duplicate-create reckoning inside the later amendment result or addressed rationale; that is too late for the pre-amendment rule.

Friction 943 confirms the same failure can also be terminal rather than pre-action: a Smoother enactment that receives an error from `pm_create_invariant`, `pm_create_*`, or another substrate authoring call must not simply end with that failed invocation as the final substantive step. If the failed call will not be followed by a grounded corrected invocation, readable target inspection, amendment, tombstone, rename, addressed mark, or other projected recovery, the enactment must record an explicit blocker/no-mutation disposition naming the failure as unrecovered and stop; silent termination after the error is not a completed Smoother response.

Friction 968 confirms the same pre-action reckoning applies when the failed substrate call was a wrong material name, schema mismatch, unavailable reached material, or similar invocation error and the practitioner intends to continue with a corrected substrate call. Before the corrected amendment, authoring call, reload, bundle wire, evaluation/invariant work, or addressed mark, make a visible recovery step that names the failed step/material/error, the corrected material or argument shape, and whether the failure changed the amendment target or basis. Retrying successfully is not itself that reckoning, and the first explanation inside the corrected write result or addressed rationale is too late.

Friction 979 confirms the same ordering applies when a Smoother failed invocation is not itself a substrate write, such as a stale material name or wrong argument shape on `read_pending_friction`, but the enactment later proceeds to substrate action or `mark_friction_addressed`. A corrected targeted read is necessary, but it is not the pre-action reckoning. Before any later substrate amendment, reload, authoring, bundle wiring, or addressed mark, record a visible recovery step that names the failed read step/material/error, the corrected read surface, and whether the failed read changed the Friction basis or chosen amendment; if no such recovery step can be made, stop with a concrete unrecovered-invocation blocker instead of continuing from the corrected read alone.

Friction 980 confirms the missing shape after a wrong-material or unavailable-material invocation is a separate pre-action recovery row, not merely the corrected retry. When a Smoother invocation fails because the selected material is not reached by the affordance, and the same enactment then intends to continue to substrate authoring, invariant/evaluation work, bundle wiring, reload, or `mark_friction_addressed`, the next visible non-retry work product must explicitly name the failed step/material/error, the corrected material that was used or will be used, and whether that failure changed the Friction basis or amendment target. A successful corrected read such as `smoother_read_pending_friction` satisfies the original read requirement only; it does not satisfy this recovery account, and no later write or closure should proceed until that account is visible or the enactment records an unrecovered-invocation blocker.

Friction 981 confirms this ordering also applies when `pm_create_invariant` or another substrate authoring call fails because the requested invariant mode, trigger, predicate, or argument value is unsupported by the projected material. If the enactment will continue to `mark_friction_addressed` without a corrected write, the closure basis must already be visible in a separate blocker/no-mutation or recovery row that names the failed step/material/error and states that no substrate mutation was applied; the first such explanation inside the addressed rationale is too late.

Friction 995 confirms that an unrecovered duplicate-create or substrate-authoring failure is not itself an addressed basis for the dispatched Friction. A blocker/no-mutation disposition may name that the failed call prevented amendment, but if it also says the failure is unrecovered and cannot ground an amend, already-addressed claim, or other projected recovery, the practitioner must stop without invoking `mark_friction_addressed` for the original Friction. Use `mark_friction_addressed` after a failed invocation only when a visible prior recovery row or current readable substrate surface supplies a real closure basis for the named Friction; do not use the mark-addressed rationale itself as the first or only place where the unrecovered failure is explained.
