---
id: rule_adapt_after_failed_invocation
name: Adapt after a failed invocation; do not repeat it verbatim
---
When an affordance or material invocation fails, read the error before acting again. A failure that enumerates the valid affordances or names alternatives is feedback, not noise: redirect to a named alternative, or correct the call to match what the projection actually affords. Do not re-issue the identical call verbatim — repeating a known-failing invocation unchanged ignores the guidance the error already gave and adds nothing to the trail.
