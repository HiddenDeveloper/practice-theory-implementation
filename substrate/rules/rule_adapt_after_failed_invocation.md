---
id: rule_adapt_after_failed_invocation
name: Reckon with failed invocations before substrate action
---
A failed invocation is not discharged by later success: before any subsequent substrate amendment, reload_seed_substrate, or addressed mark, explicitly record whether the failed call changed the chosen amendment, was irrelevant to it, or remains a blocker. Before invoking an affordance, use the material name and argument shape the active projection exposes; do not infer a material name from the affordance id, display name, or description. When an affordance or material invocation fails, read the error before acting again. A failure that enumerates the valid affordances or names alternatives is feedback, not noise: redirect to a named alternative, or correct the call to match what the projection actually affords. Do not re-issue the identical call verbatim — repeating a known-failing invocation unchanged ignores the guidance the error already gave and adds nothing to the trail. After any failed invocation, the enactment must record a follow-on reckoning before it closes: either a corrected/adapted invocation, a read that inspects the failure basis, or an explicit blocker/stop note naming why no grounded next action is possible. If the enactment continues to a substrate amendment, reload_seed_substrate, or addressed mark after a failed invocation, that reckoning must be recorded before the next substrate action or closure mark, and must explicitly name the failed call's bearing on that closure: whether the failure changed the chosen amendment, was irrelevant to it, or remains a blocker. A later successful amendment, reload, or addressed mark cannot retroactively supply the missing reckoning for an earlier failed call. For dispatched Smoother work, a failed targeted read_pending_friction call remains a blocker: reload_seed_substrate or another substrate action is not a grounding substitute unless a later successful targeted read of the same friction_id is recorded first. A later successful amendment is not by itself that reckoning. When mark_friction_addressed fails because the Friction id is not found or is already addressed, do not treat another identical empty read_pending_friction call as recovery. Record the disposition explicitly: the addressed mark did not persist because the id is absent/no longer pending/already addressed, and stop unless a distinct grounded recovery action is available. When the failed invocation is a substrate amendment whose target id does not resolve, changing to a different target id is not self-evidencing: before applying the alternate target, record the basis for that resolution through a catalog, pool, or bundle read/comparison, or stop and name the unresolved target as the blocker. A second failure does not discharge the rule; it renews the need to inspect or name the blocker rather than closing on unexplained failure.
