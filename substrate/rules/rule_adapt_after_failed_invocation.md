---
id: rule_adapt_after_failed_invocation
name: Reckon with failed invocations before substrate action
---
A failed invocation is not discharged by later success: before any subsequent substrate amendment, reload_seed_substrate, or addressed mark, explicitly record whether the failed call changed the chosen amendment, was irrelevant to it, or remains a blocker. Before invoking an affordance, use the material name and argument shape the active projection exposes; do not infer a material name from the affordance id, display name, or description. When an affordance or material invocation fails, read the error before acting again. A failure that enumerates the valid affordances or names alternatives is feedback, not noise: redirect to a named alternative, or correct the call to match what the projection actually affords. Do not re-issue the identical call verbatim — repeating a known-failing invocation unchanged ignores the guidance the error already gave and adds nothing to the trail. After any failed invocation, the enactment must record a follow-on reckoning before it closes: either a corrected/adapted invocation, a read that inspects the failure basis, or an explicit blocker/stop note naming why no grounded next action is possible. A corrected/adapted invocation only reckons with the failed call for the immediate retry path; if the enactment later proceeds to a substrate amendment, reload_seed_substrate, or addressed mark, the trail must also contain a pre-action reckoning that explicitly names the failed call's bearing on that later action or closure. That closure-bearing reckoning may be an explicit note, an intervening read/amendment argument, or another recorded step whose result or arguments make the bearing visible, but it must appear before the substrate action or addressed mark. The first appearance of that explanation inside mark_friction_addressed is too late. If the enactment continues to a substrate amendment, reload_seed_substrate, or addressed mark after a failed invocation, that reckoning must be recorded before the next substrate action or closure mark, and must explicitly name the failed call's bearing on that closure: whether the failure changed the chosen amendment, was irrelevant to it, or remains a blocker. A later successful amendment, reload, or addressed mark cannot retroactively supply the missing reckoning for an earlier failed call. For dispatched Smoother work, a failed targeted read_pending_friction call remains a blocker: reload_seed_substrate or another substrate action is not a grounding substitute unless a later successful targeted read of the same friction_id appears in this same enactment before any amendment, reload, substrate action, or addressed mark. A later successful amendment is not by itself that reckoning. When mark_friction_addressed fails because the Friction id is not found or is already addressed, do not treat another identical empty read_pending_friction call as recovery. Record the disposition explicitly: the addressed mark did not persist because the id is absent/no longer pending/already addressed, and stop unless a distinct grounded recovery action is available. When the failed invocation is a substrate amendment whose target id does not resolve, changing to a different target id is not self-evidencing: before applying the alternate target, record the basis for that resolution through a catalog, pool, or bundle read/comparison, or stop and name the unresolved target as the blocker. A second failure does not discharge the rule; it renews the need to inspect or name the blocker rather than closing on unexplained failure.
