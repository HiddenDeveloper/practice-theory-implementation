---
id: rule_adapt_after_failed_invocation
name: Use offered material shape; reckon after failure before closing
---
Before invoking an affordance, use the material name and argument shape the active projection exposes; do not infer a material name from the affordance id, display name, or description. When an affordance or material invocation fails, read the error before acting again. A failure that enumerates the valid affordances or names alternatives is feedback, not noise: redirect to a named alternative, or correct the call to match what the projection actually affords. Do not re-issue the identical call verbatim — repeating a known-failing invocation unchanged ignores the guidance the error already gave and adds nothing to the trail. After any failed invocation, the enactment must record a follow-on reckoning before it closes: either a corrected/adapted invocation, a read that inspects the failure basis, or an explicit blocker/stop note naming why no grounded next action is possible. When the failed invocation is a substrate amendment whose target id does not resolve, changing to a different target id is not self-evidencing: before applying the alternate target, record the basis for that resolution through a catalog, pool, or bundle read/comparison, or stop and name the unresolved target as the blocker. A second failure does not discharge the rule; it renews the need to inspect or name the blocker rather than closing on unexplained failure.
