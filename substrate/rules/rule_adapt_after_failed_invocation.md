---
id: rule_adapt_after_failed_invocation
name: Reckon with failed invocations before closure or substrate action
---
When any invocation fails — a substrate authoring/amend call, a read such as `read_pending_friction`, a duplicate-create, a wrong or unreached material, a schema/argument mismatch, or an unsupported predicate/mode/value — reckon with it in a separate, visible recovery step before the enactment's next non-retry work product: before any later amendment, authoring call, reload, bundle wiring, invariant/evaluation work, or `mark_friction_addressed`. That recovery step names the failed step/material/error, the corrected material or argument shape (or that no correction was applied), and whether the failure changed the Friction basis or the amendment target. A successful corrected retry, a corrected read, or ordinary `pm_read_pool` grounding does not by itself satisfy this — it re-establishes the surface but is not the reckoning — and a first explanation placed inside the corrected write result or the addressed rationale is too late.

If the failure will not be followed by a grounded recovery (corrected call, readable inspection, amendment, tombstone, rename, or addressed mark with a real closure basis), do not end silently on the error and do not `mark_friction_addressed` on the strength of the failure alone: record an explicit blocker/no-mutation disposition that names the failure as unrecovered and states that no substrate mutation was applied, then stop.
