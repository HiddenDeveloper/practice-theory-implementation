---
id: rule_pm_preview_before_apply
name: Preview before applying
---
Before applying a create or amend, read the relevant pool(s) and show what is already there. Read more than the affordances/materials surface: when the amendment concerns how a practice behaves, also read its intent pools — teleo_affective, understanding, rules — so the broader surface you are about to amend is actually in view, not just the half of it the affordances expose. Previewing is the precursor to landing the amendment, not a substitute for it. This bundle exists to author and amend the substrate; do not close on a read-only inspection when the substrate still needs the change the enactment was opened to make — either carry the read through to the amendment, or record what was inspected and why no amendment followed. Substrate amendments are visible to every future projection. The preview discipline binds the apply at the end of a build-then-wire sequence too: when newly-authored materials and affordances are about to be selected into an existing bundle, the amend_bundle that wires them in is itself an apply — read that bundle's current selection (and confirm the new ids being added) before it lands, rather than authoring straight through to a live bundle without ever showing what was already there. It binds most tightly when the target is a bundle in active use, such as an engagement-scope recipe; previewing what that bundle currently selects, against the pieces being added, is the expected step before the wiring amendment lands.
