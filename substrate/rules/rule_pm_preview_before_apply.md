---
id: rule_pm_preview_before_apply
name: Preview/read before each apply
---
Before applying a create or amend, read the relevant pool(s) and show what is already there. Any apply-side affordance in Practice Management — author_*, amend_*, reload_seed_substrate, or an amend_bundle that wires newly authored ids into a live bundle — needs a recorded preview/read in the same enactment before it is invoked, so the trail shows the basis for the change rather than only the applied mutation. This includes amend_bundle calls that change only a bundle's name or description: description-only bundle amendments are still substrate mutations and must record the current bundle state and the exact proposed field diff before the apply. The preview must include both sides needed to check the apply: the current element or bundle selection being changed, and the exact proposed new element body, field edits, or bundle-selection diff that will be sent to the apply affordance. A pool read alone is not enough when the next step creates content or rewires a bundle; the trail must expose the proposed mutation before it lands. For reload_seed_substrate specifically, the preview/read must precede the reload and must name the pending substrate effect being brought live — the changed ids/files, registry/material support, or bundle-selection change being refreshed; a reload-only enactment is not a preview. Read more than the affordances/materials surface: when the amendment concerns how a practice behaves, also read its intent pools — teleo_affective, understanding, rules — so the broader surface you are about to amend is actually in view, not just the half of it the affordances expose. Previewing is the precursor to landing the amendment, not a substitute for it. This bundle exists to author and amend the substrate; do not close on a read-only inspection when the substrate still needs the change the enactment was opened to make — either carry the read through to the amendment, or record what was inspected and why no amendment followed. Substrate amendments are visible to every future projection. The preview discipline binds the apply at the end of a build-then-wire sequence too: when newly-authored materials and affordances are about to be selected into an existing bundle, the amend_bundle that wires them in is itself an apply — read that bundle's current selection, confirm the new ids being added, and record the exact selection diff before it lands, rather than authoring straight through to a live bundle without ever showing what was already there or what will change. It binds most tightly when the target is a bundle in active use, such as an engagement-scope recipe; previewing what that bundle currently selects, against the pieces being added, is the expected step before the wiring amendment lands.
