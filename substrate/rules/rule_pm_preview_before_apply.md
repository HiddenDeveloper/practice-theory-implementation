---
id: rule_pm_preview_before_apply
name: Read pool surface before each apply
---
Before any apply — author_*, amend_*, amend_bundle, or reload_seed_substrate — record, in the same enactment, a `pm_read_pool` grounding read of the relevant current substrate surface before the apply is invoked. Choose the pool or pools that contain the ids being changed: rules/understanding/teleo_affective for intent and conduct changes, affordances/materials for projected capability changes, and the bundle-selection surface available to Practice Management before amend_bundle. Documentation-impact stewardship after reload is also grounded work: before invoking check_documentation_impact for changed substrate, read the pool surface that contains the changed ids so the documentation judgement is tied to the current substrate, not only to the reload. The same enactment must also record the exact proposed new body, field edit, or selection diff that will be sent to the apply. A generic reload or undocumented assumption about the current substrate is not a read; the trail must show `pm_read_pool` or the specific Practice Management surface that exposes the current element or selection being changed.

This binds description-only bundle amendments and the amend_bundle that wires newly-authored ids into a live bundle just as much as element edits — each is a substrate mutation visible to every future projection, and it binds most tightly on a bundle in active use such as an engagement-scope recipe. When the change concerns how a practice behaves, read its intent pools (teleo_affective, understanding, rules), not just the affordances/materials surface. For reload_seed_substrate, the preview must precede the reload and name the pending effect — the changed ids/files, registry/material support, or bundle-selection change being brought live; a reload-only enactment is not a preview. Preview is the precursor to the apply, not a substitute: either carry the read through to the amendment, or record what was inspected and why no amendment followed — do not close on a read-only inspection when the change is still owed.
