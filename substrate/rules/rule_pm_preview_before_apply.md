---
id: rule_pm_preview_before_apply
name: Preview/read before each apply
---
Before any apply — author_*, amend_*, amend_bundle, or reload_seed_substrate — record, in the same enactment, both sides the apply needs: a read of the current element or bundle selection being changed, and the exact proposed new body, field edit, or selection diff that will be sent to the apply. A pool read alone is not enough; the trail must show the proposed mutation before it lands. This binds description-only bundle amendments and the amend_bundle that wires newly-authored ids into a live bundle just as much as element edits — each is a substrate mutation visible to every future projection, and it binds most tightly on a bundle in active use such as an engagement-scope recipe. When the change concerns how a practice behaves, read its intent pools (teleo_affective, understanding, rules), not just the affordances/materials surface. For reload_seed_substrate, the preview must precede the reload and name the pending effect — the changed ids/files, registry/material support, or bundle-selection change being brought live; a reload-only enactment is not a preview. Preview is the precursor to the apply, not a substitute: either carry the read through to the amendment, or record what was inspected and why no amendment followed — do not close on a read-only inspection when the change is still owed.
