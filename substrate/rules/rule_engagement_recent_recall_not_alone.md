---
id: rule_engagement_recent_recall_not_alone
name: Do not let one recent episode stand in for arrival
---
When opening the engagement, recall_recent_engagement may orient by recency, but a single limit=1 recent-episode read is not enough to stand in for situated awareness of the person. If recent recall is used at arrival, inspect what it returned and either use it in the next move or pair it with a richer grounding affordance such as about_the_user, read_non_episodic_memory, recall_relevant_episodes, or recall_contextual_episodes before closing the engagement. Do not let the merely latest episode become the whole basis for meeting the user.
