---
id: rule_morning_briefing_unread_first
name: 'Opening gate: unread mail, then configured sites'
---
Begin each morning briefing by grounding the source set before any summary, individual source work, optional live snapshot, browser site check, or final assembly. First invoke the email-reading surface for unread mail, reaching `gmail_user_search_threads` through `read_user_email` for actual messages; where the material surface or user-supplied context distinguishes accounts, attempt the unread read account by account. If mail cannot be read, name the unread-mail access or data gap instead of assuming an empty inbox. Then invoke the configured site-list surface, reaching `read_morning_briefing_sites` through `read_morning_site_list`, before checking individual sites or assembling the briefing. Do not invoke one-off site-check surfaces, including `morning_briefing_browser_site_check`, until both source-gathering reads are present on the trail, or until a concrete access, auth, configuration, or data blocker has been recorded for the missing read. For each enabled usual site, either capture a site check through the available site-check surface or record the access gap plainly. Treat these two source reads as both a site-check gate and an assembly gate, not optional preparation: a morning briefing enactment is not ready to invoke `morning_briefing_browser_site_check` for direct site work or `morning_briefing_assemble_report` for final assembly until the trail contains `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail and `read_morning_briefing_sites` for the configured recurring sites, or until the practitioner has recorded a concrete access, auth, configuration, or data blocker for each missing source. Do not use direct one-off site checks, live snapshots, browser checks, or the final briefing artifact as substitutes for the unread-mail read and configured-site-list grounding. If the briefing must proceed with a missing source surface, the site-check or final assembly must explicitly name that missing surface and the gap that prevented it.

If a morning briefing pass has already begun with site-list work, browser site checks, a live snapshot, or source interpretation before the unread-mail read, stop expanding the briefing and immediately backfill `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail before any further source interpretation, site-check synthesis, or final assembly. A successful `read_morning_briefing_sites` step does not partially satisfy the email side of the gate; the pass remains ungrounded until the Gmail unread read is present on the trail or a concrete Gmail access/data/material blocker is recorded. This amendment responds to Friction 554: the quality signal showed repeated recent morning briefing passes still omitting `gmail_user_search_threads`, including a pass that read the configured site list but left unread mail ungrounded.

Friction 579 confirms the remaining quality failure is specifically unread-email omission across the evaluated daily-orientation window, even when site grounding is mixed rather than wholly absent. Treat any current-enactment morning briefing without `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail, or without a concrete Gmail access/data/material blocker, as insufficiently grounded regardless of site-list reads, browser site checks, live snapshots, or final assembly quality. Mixed or successful site grounding never compensates for the missing unread-mail source read; backfill the unread Gmail read before continuing, or name the Gmail gap as a limiting source gap in the briefing.

Friction 583 confirms the source gate is still being missed across the practice-quality window, including enactments that omitted unread email entirely and others that omitted both unread email and the configured site-list read. Treat the two recurring source reads as an opening checklist for every morning briefing pass, not as work that can be deferred until assembly: before any briefing answer, source interpretation, live snapshot, browser check, or closure, the current trail must show an attempted `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` unread-mail read and an attempted `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` configured-site-list read, or a concrete access, auth, configuration, data, or material blocker for each missing source. If either recurring source read is absent, the enactment is unfinished; perform the missing read before continuing, or close only with an explicit limiting gap that says the briefing could not be fully grounded.

Friction 601 confirms the repeated drift has persisted after the source gate was documented: recent morning briefing passes still recorded no `gmail_user_search_threads`, often recorded no `read_morning_briefing_sites`, and in one case used only an invalid placeholder browser check. Make the opening sequence mechanical at the start of every `morning_briefing` enactment: step 1 is `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail or a concrete Gmail access/data/material blocker; step 2 is `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for the configured recurring site list or a concrete configuration/access/data/material blocker. Until those two trail facts are present, do not proceed to live market snapshots, browser site checks, source interpretation, or final assembly. A placeholder, invalid, ad hoc, or one-off `morning_briefing_browser_site_check` result never satisfies the configured-site-list read and should be treated as evidence the pass must stop and backfill the site list before continuing.

Friction 630 confirms the same source-grounding gap is still recurring across the latest evaluated window: all three recent morning briefing enactments lacked `gmail_user_search_threads`, and two also lacked `read_morning_briefing_sites`. Treat this as a hard pre-closure gate as well as an opening sequence. Before any morning briefing enactment offers orientation, source interpretation, action/watch/routine grouping, or a final answer, inspect the current trail for both recurring source reads. If either `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` or `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` is absent and no concrete access, auth, configuration, data, or material blocker for that exact source has been recorded, the next move is the missing read, not additional briefing work or closure. A pass that has only one of the two reads is still unfinished; it must backfill the other source or explicitly close as unable to produce a fully grounded briefing because that source was blocked.

Friction 634 confirms the same practice-quality failure after those gates were present: across three evaluated morning briefing enactments, every pass still lacked `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads`, and two also lacked `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites`. Treat this as failed source coverage even when the pass never reaches the final assembly material and even when one source or a browser check produced some output. If either required recurring source read is missing and no concrete blocker for that exact source has been recorded, the only valid next move is to perform the missing read; if the read is blocked, record the blocker and explicitly limit the briefing as not fully grounded rather than presenting the routine as complete.

Friction 644 confirms the source-gate failure is still live across the latest evaluated three-pass window: unread email was not read in any pass, and two passes also skipped the configured recurring-site list before moving into other site-check activity. Treat the first two source reads as a stop-the-line checklist at the beginning of the pass and again before any handoff or closure. If the trail lacks `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail, the immediate next move is that Gmail unread read or a concrete Gmail access/auth/data/material blocker; do not proceed from a site-list read, browser site check, live snapshot, or source interpretation while Gmail remains absent. If the trail lacks `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites`, the immediate next move after Gmail grounding is the configured-site-list read or a concrete site-list configuration/access/data/material blocker. A morning briefing pass with only one recurring source read, only direct browser checks, or only an invalid/ad hoc site check is unfinished and must either backfill the missing source reads or explicitly close as unable to provide a fully grounded briefing because the named source surface is blocked.
