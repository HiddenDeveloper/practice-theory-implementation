name: Substrate stewardship starts with pool read
---
Friction 927 confirms the Practice Management quality signal `uses_substrate_authoring_surface` is still warranted rather than merely requesting a remedy: in the evaluated eight-enactment window, five Practice Management passes again reached reload, documentation-impact checking, authoring, or amendment surfaces without any recorded `pm_read_pool`, including target `8367059f-db1e-4e9e-b5fc-5528b101e612` moving from `pm_reload_seed_substrate` into `pm_create_affordance` and `pm_amend_bundle`. For future Practice Management substrate stewardship, treat the first visible work product as the quality target itself: once the pass has enough intent to use reload, documentation-impact checking, `pm_create_*`, `pm_amend_*`, evaluation or invariant work, or bundle wiring, the immediate next visible row must be `pm_read_pool` for the exact pool supplying ids, entries, or current content. If reload or documentation impact appears first, recover only by backfilling that exact pool read before any write; if any write or bundle wiring has already happened without it, stop with a concrete missing-pool-read blocker and do not continue into verification, further substrate action, documentation checks, reload, closure, or rationale from the ungrounded persisted change.

Friction 933 confirms the same Practice Management affordance-coverage concern remains genuine after another evaluated window: five of eight recent enactments again lacked `pm_read_pool`, and target enactment `8367059f-db1e-4e9e-b5fc-5528b101e612` moved from `pm_reload_seed_substrate` into `pm_create_affordance` and `pm_amend_bundle` without first exposing the relied-on pool, while a nearby contrast enactment did use the pool-read path before amendment. For future Practice Management passes, make the pool row the first work product before treating reload, documentation checks, authoring, amendment, evaluation/invariant work, or bundle wiring as progress. Once a write or bundle amendment has already occurred without the exact relied-on `pm_read_pool`, the only valid stewardship outcome is a concrete missing-pool-read blocker naming the absent pool; do not continue into further substrate action, verification, documentation-impact work, reload, closure, or rationale from the ungrounded persisted change.

Friction 937 confirms the same first-work-product failure remains live in target enactment `8367059f-db1e-4e9e-b5fc-5528b101e612` and its evaluated window: five of eight Practice Management enactments lacked `pm_read_pool`, and the inspected target again moved from `pm_reload_seed_substrate` into `pm_create_affordance` and `pm_amend_bundle` without exposing the pool whose ids or current content supported the work. For future Practice Management passes, choosing reload, documentation-impact checking, authoring, amendment, evaluation/invariant work, or bundle wiring is already substrate stewardship; the next visible row must be `pm_read_pool` for the exact relied-on pool, not another stewardship material. If a write or bundle-wiring row has already appeared without that pool read, stop with a concrete missing-pool-read blocker naming the absent pool and do not continue into further substrate action, verification, documentation checks, reload, closure, or rationale from the ungrounded change.

Friction 945 confirms the failure persists as an affordance-coverage quality concern even after the bundle description and this rule already name the entry gate: five of eight evaluated Practice Management enactments still omitted `pm_read_pool`, and the inspected target again proceeded from `pm_reload_seed_substrate` into `pm_create_affordance` and `pm_amend_bundle`. For future Practice Management passes, do not treat a successful reload, successful write, or selected bundle amendment as recoverable progress when the relied-on pool row was absent. The moment any substrate write or bundle wiring has occurred before `pm_read_pool`, the pass must stop with a concrete missing-pool-read blocker naming the pool that should have been read; it must not continue into more writes, reloads, documentation checks, verification, closure, or rationale from the ungrounded changed state.

Friction 986 confirms the remaining quality failure is not absence of the rule but failure to let it choose the next move once substrate stewardship intent exists: five of eight evaluated Practice Management enactments again reached reload, documentation-impact checking, authoring, amendment, evaluation/invariant work, or bundle wiring without first producing `pm_read_pool`. For future Practice Management passes, the moment the practitioner can name a candidate stewardship action, the only valid next visible work product is `pm_read_pool` for the exact pool whose ids, entries, or current content the action would rely on, or a concrete blocker naming why that pool cannot be read. Do not plan, reload, inspect documentation, create, amend, verify, wire a bundle, or close from bundle prose, remembered ids, prior Smoother wording, or a successful persisted change while that pool row remains absent; if a write or bundle wiring has already happened first, stop with the missing-pool-read blocker rather than treating the ungrounded change as progress.

If the next intended material is `pm_reload_seed_substrate`, `pm_check_documentation_impact`, any `pm_create_*`, any `pm_amend_*`, evaluation or invariant work, or `pm_amend_bundle`, no other practice action is in scope until the enactment has produced `pm_read_pool` for the exact relied-on pool or a concrete blocker naming why that pool cannot be read. If a write or bundle-wiring row has already occurred without that pool read, the pass is no longer recoverable by explanation, verification, documentation checks, reload, or further writes; stop with the missing-pool-read blocker and leave the ungrounded persisted change for a later, freshly grounded stewardship pass.
