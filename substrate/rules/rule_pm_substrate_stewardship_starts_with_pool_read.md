id: rule_pm_substrate_stewardship_starts_with_pool_read
name: Substrate stewardship starts with pool read
---
Friction 797 confirms the Practice Management quality concern is still genuine after repeated rule, bundle, and invariant guidance: five of eight recent stewardship enactments again reached reload, documentation-impact, authoring, amendment, or bundle-wiring surfaces without `pm_read_pool`, while a nearby contrast pass showed the read path was reachable. Treat `pm_read_pool` as the first work product of the pass, not background guidance and not something later persistence can satisfy. As soon as a Practice Management pass recognizes that it may reload, check documentation impact, create, amend, author/amend evaluations or invariants, or wire a bundle, the immediate next visible move must be `pm_read_pool` for the exact relied-on pool; if any other stewardship material has already appeared first, the pass is interrupted until that pool read is backfilled before writes, or until a concrete missing-pool-read blocker is recorded once a write or bundle wiring has already happened.

Friction 804 confirms the same Practice Management affordance-coverage concern remains genuine in the inspected window: five of eight evaluated enactments reached reload, documentation-impact checking, substrate authoring, or bundle wiring without `pm_read_pool`, while comparable authoring enactments did record the pool read. Treat this as failure to obey the selected rule, not lack of description. For future Practice Management substrate stewardship, once any stewardship material appears before the relied-on pool read, the pass is in recovery only: if no write or bundle wiring has occurred, backfill the exact `pm_read_pool` as the next visible move; if a write or `pm_amend_bundle` has already occurred, stop further stewardship and record the missing-pool-read blocker. Do not continue from reload, documentation-impact results, persisted writes, invariant coverage, bundle prose, or remembered ids into verification, more authoring, bundle wiring, or closure while that blocker is unresolved.

Friction 811 confirms this remains a practice-quality concern in a later evaluated window, including an enactment that moved from `pm_reload_seed_substrate` into `pm_create_affordance` and `pm_amend_bundle` without any `pm_read_pool`. Treat that sequence as an already-breached stewardship pass, not as a pass that can be repaired by another write or by citing the bundle description. Once reload has been followed by authoring, amendment, evaluation or invariant work, documentation-impact checking, or bundle wiring without the relevant pool read, the next Practice Management outcome must be a concrete missing-pool-read blocker naming the relied-on pool that was absent; do not perform further substrate action, verification, reload, documentation checks, or closure that presents the write as completed stewardship.

Friction 843 confirms the remaining Practice Management failure is a second-row failure after stewardship is already recognizable: five of eight evaluated enactments still omitted `pm_read_pool`, including reload-only closures and a reload-to-author/wire sequence, while nearby authoring work showed the pool-read row is available. For future Practice Management passes, once the practitioner has enough intent to choose any stewardship surface, the next visible row must be the exact `pm_read_pool` for the relied-on pool before any other stewardship row or closure. A pass may not use reload, documentation impact, local file inspection, successful persistence, invariant coverage, or bundle prose as a bridge to the pool read; if the pool read is not the next row, the pass must either backfill it immediately before any write, or, after any write or bundle wiring has occurred, stop with a concrete missing-pool-read blocker and no further verification or closure that presents the stewardship as complete.

Friction 857 confirms the same evaluated-window quality concern while also narrowing its interpretation: the missing-window count can include reload-only cases, and those should not be treated as the same misconduct as a write-bearing authoring pass. Preserve that distinction in future Practice Management work. A reload-only or documentation-only pass that has not read a relied-on pool is limited and unfinished if it tries to present substrate stewardship as complete; it should backfill the exact `pm_read_pool` before any write or closure that relies on current substrate, or record a narrow reload/documentation surface limit. A pass that has already moved from reload or documentation into `pm_create_*`, `pm_amend_*`, evaluation or invariant work, or `pm_amend_bundle` without the relied-on pool read is the breached stewardship case: stop further substrate action and record a concrete missing-pool-read blocker naming the absent pool, rather than using the persisted write, reload result, documentation context, bundle prose, or invariant coverage as closure.
