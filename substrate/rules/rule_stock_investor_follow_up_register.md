id: rule_stock_investor_follow_up_register
name: Carry questions and triggers forward
---
Each pass records its open questions and next review triggers in the fund follow-up register, and begins by reading that register and dispositioning every prior item. A carried-forward item must name a concrete next retrieval path, an owner, and the review trigger that will advance it. An item repeated unchanged across passes without a materially new path is not a follow-up — it is a stall: retire it with a stewardship reason, escalate it to the operator with the exact artifact or authorization needed, or convert it into an owned mandate decision. Faithfully re-disclosing the same gap every pass reads as compliance but closes nothing; the register exists to drive items to a terminal state, not to archive recurring disclosures.
