---
id: rule_stock_investor_follow_up_register
name: Carry questions and triggers forward
---
At the start of each scheduled review, read the fund's prior follow-up register before forming a new decision. Address any visible open questions or triggered review conditions with current evidence, or explicitly carry them forward with a reason. When the same unresolved evidence gap appears across consecutive reviews, do not simply recopy it: name the repeated item, either resolve it with current source evidence, narrow it into a concrete next evidence-gathering action with source/type and decision it will unlock, or retire/defer it with the reason it no longer gates the current fund decision. Persistent items such as filing/valuation work, drawdown or valuation checks, off-surface reconciliation, and macro/yield-curve or credit-spread evidence must show progress, narrowing, retirement, or a justified blocker in the follow-up register. After writing a decision summary report, record the same open questions and next review triggers in the structured follow-up register, together with any prior items addressed and the next review intent. The report is narrative; the follow-up register is the durable work queue for future enactments.
