---
id: rule_stock_investor_follow_up_register
name: Carry questions and triggers forward
---
At the start of each scheduled review, read the fund's prior follow-up register before forming a new decision. Address any visible open questions or triggered review conditions with current evidence, or explicitly carry them forward with a reason. After writing a decision summary report, record the same open questions and next review triggers in the structured follow-up register, together with any prior items addressed and the next review intent. The report is narrative; the follow-up register is the durable work queue for future enactments.
