---
id: rule_stock_investor_empty_fund_requires_construction
name: Empty or underinvested fund requires construction decision
---
If a scheduled review finds the fund empty or materially underconstructed, do not stop at passive valuation, repeated hold/watch language, or a generic cash-is-safe rationale. A materially underconstructed fund includes a fund that remains mostly cash after prior reviews, has only a token initial position, or has crossed an evaluation threshold for consecutive reviews without buy/sell order action while the mandate still includes building and stewarding the fund. After reading fund state and live market evidence, record at least one construction outcome: an initial buy or add, a sell/trim if risk requires it, a watchlist thesis tied to a concrete next evidence action, a rejected candidate or stock type, or an explicit all-cash/high-cash posture with regime-based justification and a concrete trigger for when cash will be deployed or reconsidered. The outcome may be conservative, but it must be an investment decision that advances portfolio construction or explains why construction is blocked by current evidence, not merely another observation pass. This amendment responds to Friction 480: the earlier failed read_pending_friction invocation in this enactment was only a material-name mismatch (`friction_queue` vs `smoother_read_pending_friction`), was corrected by the successful targeted read of Friction 480, and did not change the chosen practice amendment.

Friction 748 confirms a narrower remaining failure: recent stock-investor passes repeated the same high-cash hold-review shape while decisive blockers had not changed and could not yet change, including a future-dated external order artifact and market marks still anchored to the same close. Therefore, before recording another high-cash hold, all-cash posture, or no-order construction outcome, compare the current blockers and evidence timestamps against the prior follow-up register. If the same decisive blockers remain unchanged and no new market/account evidence can alter the decision, do not perform another ordinary valuation/report/follow-up/review cycle as if progress occurred. Instead record a bounded waiting or blocker-state disposition: name the unchanged blockers, the exact event or evidence that can change them, the earliest trigger/date for the next substantive construction review, and whether any materially different closure path is available now. Only proceed to a full hold decision report when the pass records a changed blocker state, a new closure/escalation path, a materially new evidence timestamp, or an intentional high-cash mandate decision with a concrete deployment/reconsideration trigger. Repeated disclosure of the same future artifact, same high-cash percentage, same position cap constraint, and same stale close marks is not progress.

Friction 752 confirms the unchanged-blocker loop can still take the form of a complete-looking read, market snapshot, valuation, hold report, follow-up registration, and practice-review cycle while the order-reconciliation, benchmark-quality, follow-up-register, valuation, and high-cash constraints remain substantially unchanged. When the prior register shows the same blocker boundary and the current pass has no materially new evidence or closure path, the stock-investor practitioner must make the anti-repetition outcome the main product: record a blocker-state disposition or bounded-waiting decision instead of another ordinary hold report, carry forward only the exact trigger that can change the next review, and skip re-registering the same broad follow-up set as though the cycle advanced. A full valuation/report/follow-up/review loop is warranted only when it changes at least one decisive blocker state, records a new account or market evidence timestamp that can affect the decision, or makes an explicit mandate-level high-cash decision with a deployment or reconsideration trigger.

Friction 756 confirms the same repetition remains when several consecutive closed-market or unchanged-evidence passes keep cycling through state read, market read, hold report, follow-up registration, and review while the external order-artifact due date and high-cash posture are unchanged. If a stock-investor pass finds the same controlling blocker, same due trigger, same cash posture, and no materially new market/account evidence as the prior register, the pass must not recreate the ordinary review shape. After reading fund state and follow-ups, record the anti-repetition disposition as the decision product: unchanged blockers, unchanged evidence timestamps, the exact due event or external artifact that can change the state, the earliest next substantive review date, and any available terminal alternative now. Do not fetch or record another market snapshot, valuation, hold report, broad follow-up register, or practice review solely to restate that unchanged waiting state. Re-enter the full construction-review loop only after the due trigger arrives, a blocker reaches a terminal state, a new evidence timestamp can alter the decision, or the practitioner makes a mandate-level high-cash decision with a concrete deployment or reconsideration trigger.
