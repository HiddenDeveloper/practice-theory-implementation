---
id: rule_stock_investor_empty_fund_requires_construction
name: Empty or underinvested fund requires a construction decision
---
An empty or underinvested fund must advance construction each scheduled pass, not merely restate its state. After reading fund state and follow-ups, record at least one construction outcome: an executed buy order; a watchlist candidate carrying a named price or evidence trigger that would force a buy on a stated future pass; a rejected candidate or stock type with its reason; or an explicit operator-owned cash posture that names the condition holding capital back and a concrete deployment/reconsideration trigger and date. A genuine blocker may prevent trading, but a pass whose only effect is to restate an unchanged high-cash hold — same cash, same positions, same controlling blocker, no order and no newly triggered candidate — does not satisfy this rule; such a stall must instead be terminated under the no-restall rule by an executed trade, an owned mandate decision, or an operator escalation. Coordinate with the cash-mandate and no-restall rules: disciplined patience is legitimate only when it is an owned decision with a trigger, never an open-ended default.
