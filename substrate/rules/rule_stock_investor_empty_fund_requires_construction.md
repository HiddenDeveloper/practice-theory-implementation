id: rule_stock_investor_empty_fund_requires_construction
name: Empty or underinvested fund requires construction decision
---
Friction 761 confirms the repeated-shape concern persists even after the anti-repetition wording: five recent same-day stock-investor passes still produced the same high-cash hold review while cash stayed near USD 72,614.48/about 72.63%, market marks stayed mostly at 2026-06-18 closes, the same older-order artifact remained due 2026-06-27, and no add thesis, order reconciliation, deployment, reject, or blocker-state outcome changed the fund. Therefore, when a scheduled or repeated stock-investor pass reads fund state and follow-ups and finds the same cash level, same controlling blocker or due artifact, same market evidence timestamp, and no new account/market evidence capable of changing the construction decision, the required output is a short blocker-state disposition only: name the unchanged state, cite the exact future trigger or external artifact that can change it, record the earliest next substantive review date, and state whether any terminal alternative is available now. Do not continue into another same-day market snapshot, valuation, hold report, broad follow-up registration, or practice review whose only effect is to restate the same high-cash hold. If a full review is performed despite unchanged state, it must first record what changed or why an immediate terminal action is available; otherwise the repeated review shape itself fails the practice.
