---
id: rule_stock_investor_empty_fund_requires_construction
name: Empty fund requires construction decision
---
If a scheduled review finds the fund empty, do not stop at a passive valuation. After reading live market evidence, record at least one construction outcome: an initial buy, a watchlist thesis, a rejected candidate or stock type, or an explicit all-cash posture with regime-based justification. The outcome may be conservative, but it must be an investment decision rather than merely noting that no prior positions exist.
