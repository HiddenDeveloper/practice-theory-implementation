id: rule_stock_investor_empty_fund_requires_construction
name: Empty or underinvested fund requires construction decision
---
If a scheduled review finds the fund empty or materially underconstructed, do not stop at passive valuation, repeated hold/watch language, or a generic cash-is-safe rationale. A materially underconstructed fund includes a fund that remains mostly cash after prior reviews, has only a token initial position, or has crossed an evaluation threshold for consecutive reviews without buy/sell order action while the mandate still includes building and stewarding the fund. After reading fund state and live market evidence, record at least one construction outcome: an initial buy or add, a sell/trim if risk requires it, a watchlist thesis tied to a concrete next evidence action, a rejected candidate or stock type, or an explicit all-cash/high-cash posture with regime-based justification and a concrete trigger for when cash will be deployed or reconsidered. The outcome may be conservative, but it must be an investment decision that advances portfolio construction or explains why construction is blocked by current evidence, not merely another observation pass. This amendment responds to Friction 480: the earlier failed read_pending_friction invocation in this enactment was only a material-name mismatch (`friction_queue` vs `smoother_read_pending_friction`), was corrected by the successful targeted read of Friction 480, and did not change the chosen practice amendment.
