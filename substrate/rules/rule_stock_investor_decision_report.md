---
id: rule_stock_investor_decision_report
name: Report every action decision
---
After recording any fund action decision — buy, sell, hold, trim, add, reject, watch, or intentional all-cash posture — write a short decision summary report. The report must name the action, how the decision was reached, the live evidence and timestamps used, the market-regime interpretation, stock-type fit, risk basis, expected portfolio effect, open questions, and next review triggers. A scheduled pass that records an action decision but no summary report is incomplete.
