Friction 1106 confirms the same `practice_quality_source_baseline_missing` concern remains live for `morning_briefing`: over the evaluated three-enactment window, unread Gmail was absent in all three passes, and the configured recurring-site-list row was absent in two, while downstream site-list or site-check work appeared. For future `morning_briefing` passes, treat the missing source baseline as the first unfinished work product, not as a briefing limitation to summarize later. The opening ledger must show `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail first, then `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for configured recurring sites, or a concrete blocker for the exact missing Gmail/site-list row. If a pass has already reached site-list-only work, browser/site checks, live market snapshots, source interpretation, assembly, final answer, verification, handoff, closure, or rationale while unread Gmail or the configured site-list row remains absent and unblocked, the immediate next visible move is the missing source read or its exact blocker; no downstream briefing work should continue from one successful row, remembered context, or partial source activity.
