name: No source read means the briefing has not started
---
Friction 706 confirms the same `reads_morning_sources_before_assembling` quality concern persists after prior source-gate wording: in the latest evaluated three-enactment window, unread Gmail was not read in any run, and the configured morning site list was skipped in two of three runs, with no concrete source blockers replacing those reads. Treat this as a first-work-product requirement rather than a reminder. A `morning_briefing` pass earns no briefing progress from a configured-site-list-only read, a direct Guardian/browser site check, an invalid URL site-check gap, live snapshot, supplied context, or final prose while either recurring source read is absent. The first visible briefing work must establish unread Gmail through `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` or a concrete Gmail access/auth/data/material blocker; the second must establish the configured site list through `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` or a concrete configuration/access/data/material blocker. If any other source, site-check, snapshot, synthesis, handoff, assembly, or final-answer step appears before both source facts or blockers are visible, stop that line immediately and backfill Gmail first, then the configured site list. If either source cannot be backfilled, close only by explicitly naming the blocked source surface and stating that the briefing did not reach its full grounding threshold; do not present partial source work as a completed morning orientation.

Friction 721 confirms the miss is still live in the next evaluated window: unread mail was absent in all three evaluated `morning_briefing` enactments, and two of those also lacked the configured usual-site-list read before other site-level work or briefing progress. Therefore the next `morning_briefing` pass must make the two-source ledger the first visible work product before any site check, live snapshot, source interpretation, assembly, handoff, or final answer: (1) `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail, or a concrete Gmail access/auth/data/material blocker; (2) `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for configured usual sites, or a concrete site-list configuration/access/data/material blocker. If the trail shows site-list-only progress, browser-site progress, invalid URL gaps, supplied context, or assembly while either ledger row is absent and unblocked, the pass is unfinished; the immediate next move is to backfill Gmail first and then the configured site list, or explicitly close as not fully grounded because the named source surface is blocked.
