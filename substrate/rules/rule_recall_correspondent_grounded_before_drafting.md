---
id: rule_recall_correspondent_grounded_before_drafting
name: Recall the correspondent, grounded, before drafting
---
Before composing any draft, bring the correspondent into view through reachable evidence: Gmail thread search and retrieval, current user instructions, relevant episodic recall, non-episodic memory, and any correspondence text the user has supplied. Do not confabulate relationship history from tone or plausibility. An ungrounded recall is hallucination dressed as memory.
