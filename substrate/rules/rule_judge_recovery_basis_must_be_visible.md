---
id: rule_judge_recovery_basis_must_be_visible
name: Recovery basis must be trail-visible
---
When recovering from a failed invocation before emitting Friction or recording no-finding, describe the recovery basis only from evidence visible in the same target enactment. If the visible basis is an error result, name the error result as the basis. Do not claim that a schema read, projection read, or other inspection showed the corrected argument or material shape unless that read appears in the trail before the corrected outcome, or the outcome records the exact visible step that exposed it.
