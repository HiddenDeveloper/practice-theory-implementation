---
id: rule_pm_pool_read_first_or_stop
name: Read the pool first or stop
---
Friction 1111 confirms the Practice Management `uses_substrate_authoring_surface` quality concern remains genuine after another evaluated window: five of eight evaluated Practice Management enactments reached reload, documentation-impact checking, authoring, amendment, evaluation/invariant, or bundle-wiring surfaces without the required `pm_read_pool`, while a nearby comparison enactment showed `pm_read_pool` before amendment was reachable. For future Practice Management passes, treat the first substrate-stewardship surface as a hard gate, not guidance to repair later. If the next intended move is `pm_reload_seed_substrate`, `pm_check_documentation_impact`, any `pm_create_*`, any `pm_amend_*`, evaluation/invariant work, or `pm_amend_bundle`, the immediately prior visible stewardship work product must be `pm_read_pool` for the exact pool supplying the ids, entries, or current content. If reload or documentation-impact appears first, the only permitted continuation is that exact pool read or a concrete pool-read blocker. If any create, amend, evaluation/invariant write, or bundle-wiring row has already occurred without the relied-on pool read, stop with a concrete missing-pool-read blocker and do not continue into further substrate action, verification, reload, documentation checks, bundle wiring, or closure from the ungrounded change.

Friction 1115 confirms the same Practice Management failure in the concrete authoring path from enactment `8367059f-db1e-4e9e-b5fc-5528b101e612`: `pm_reload_seed_substrate` was followed by `pm_create_affordance` for `read_morning_site_list` and `pm_amend_bundle` for `morning_briefing` without any `pm_read_pool`. Treat that sequence as already interrupted at reload and failed at the first write. The only valid continuation after reload is the relevant `pm_read_pool`; after `pm_create_*` or `pm_amend_bundle` has occurred without that row, Practice Management must stop with a concrete missing-pool-read blocker and must not use the persisted affordance, bundle amendment, documentation context, reload context, or closure rationale as recovery for the ungrounded write.

Friction 1125 confirms the same affordance-coverage failure remains a Practice Management quality concern after the practice surface already names `pm_read_pool` as the entry gate: five of eight evaluated enactments still lacked the pool row, including target `8367059f-db1e-4e9e-b5fc-5528b101e612` moving from `pm_reload_seed_substrate` into authoring and bundle amendment. For future Practice Management passes, treat the absence of `pm_read_pool` before the first stewardship surface as the pass's controlling outcome, not as context for more stewardship. If reload or documentation-impact has appeared first, recover only by making the exact relied-on `pm_read_pool` the next visible row before any write; once `pm_create_*`, `pm_amend_*`, evaluation/invariant write, or `pm_amend_bundle` has occurred without that row, the pass must stop with a concrete missing-pool-read blocker naming the absent pool and ungrounded material, and must not continue into verification, reload, documentation checks, further writes, bundle wiring, or closure from the persisted change.
