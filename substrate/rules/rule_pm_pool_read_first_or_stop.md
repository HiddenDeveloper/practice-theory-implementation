name: Read the pool first or stop
---
Friction 1111 confirms the Practice Management `uses_substrate_authoring_surface` quality concern remains genuine after another evaluated window: five of eight evaluated Practice Management enactments reached reload, documentation-impact checking, authoring, amendment, evaluation/invariant, or bundle-wiring surfaces without the required `pm_read_pool`, while a nearby comparison enactment showed `pm_read_pool` before amendment was reachable. For future Practice Management passes, treat the first substrate-stewardship surface as a hard gate, not guidance to repair later. If the next intended move is `pm_reload_seed_substrate`, `pm_check_documentation_impact`, any `pm_create_*`, any `pm_amend_*`, evaluation/invariant work, or `pm_amend_bundle`, the immediately prior visible stewardship work product must be `pm_read_pool` for the exact pool supplying the ids, entries, or current content. If reload or documentation-impact appears first, the only permitted continuation is that exact pool read or a concrete pool-read blocker. If any create, amend, evaluation/invariant write, or bundle-wiring row has already occurred without the relied-on pool read, stop with a concrete missing-pool-read blocker and do not continue into further substrate action, verification, reload, documentation checks, bundle wiring, or closure from the ungrounded change.
