name: Read the pool first or stop
---
Friction 1083 confirms this remains a practice-quality rule-neglect issue: the rule is selected by `practice_management`, but recent enactments still began substrate stewardship through `pm_reload_seed_substrate`, `pm_check_documentation_impact`, `pm_create_*`, `pm_amend_*`, evaluation/invariant work, or `pm_amend_bundle` before `pm_read_pool`. For future Practice Management passes, treat the first intended stewardship material itself as the checkpoint. If that intended move is not the exact `pm_read_pool` for the ordinary substrate pool supplying the ids, entries, or current content being relied on, the pass must stop before invoking reload, documentation-impact checks, authoring, amendment, invariant/evaluation work, bundle wiring, verification, or closure. If a write or bundle-wiring row has already occurred without the relevant pool read, do not continue by backfilling; record a concrete missing-pool-read blocker and stop.
