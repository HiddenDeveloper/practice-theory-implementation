---
id: rule_smoother_mark_when_done
name: Ground before marking; non-Friction routed items are blockers
---
When a Smoother closure rationale asserts that an existing substrate surface already carries the requirement, material guidance, or behavioral content needed for one of the Friction's named targets, inspect that exact current surface in the same enactment before marking addressed. Use the available read that exposes the relied-on surface, such as the affordance, material, pool element, bundle selection, or governed invariant surface, and make the inspected id plus the relied-on prose or field visible in the trail or in the addressed rationale. If a rationale relies on current governed invariants by id, the trail must expose the invariant content, predicate, trigger, message, or other cited field before those invariants can supply an already-addressed/no-mutation basis. If no afforded read exposes the relied-on surface, record a basis-unreadable blocker or apply a substantive amendment that makes the requirement visible; do not close from an unsupported assertion that the substrate already covered it.

Friction 711 confirms this applies to duplicate-create or already-exists invariant paths: failed `pm_create_invariant` attempts do not expose the existing invariant record's active trigger, predicate, message, status, or content. A Smoother may not mark a Friction addressed by claiming those invariant details unless a same-enactment successful read or amendment result has made the cited invariant fields visible. If the current projection has no read surface for the existing invariant record, treat the invariant basis as unreadable and either apply a substantive amendment that does not depend on the unseen invariant fields, or stop with an explicit blocker/no-mutation rationale instead of closing from the unsupported invariant claim.

Friction 880 confirms the same grounding requirement applies when the addressed rationale cites same-enactment discovery, projected affordance guidance, schema exposure, or the absence of a readable substrate surface. Those claimed bases must appear as visible prior steps before `mark_friction_addressed`: for example, the discovery/read step that exposed the affordance or schema, the projected affordance guidance being relied on, or the failed/empty read that establishes no readable surface. If the trail has not exposed that basis, do not close by naming it inside the addressed rationale; either perform a substantive amendment grounded in visible prior steps or stop with a blocker/no-mutation disposition that names the basis as unreadable.

Friction 907 confirms that a pool-level entry is not by itself an active practice surface. When a no-mutation closure relies on an existing pool element to answer a Friction about a named practice bundle, the same enactment must expose that the affected bundle selects the relied-on element, or apply a bundle amendment that wires it in. A `read_pool` row showing the element exists in the global pool does not ground that the practice has inherited it; if no bundle-selection read or bundle amendment surface is available, stop with a basis-unreadable blocker instead of marking the Friction addressed from pool presence alone.
