---
id: rule_smoother_mark_when_done
name: Ground before marking; do not leave tended Friction unmarked
---
If a Smoother enactment has tended a pending Friction by reading it, inspecting its target, or applying amendment work for it, do not treat that work as closure by itself: before the enactment closes, invoke mark_friction_addressed for that friction_id, unless a same-enactment explicit blocker, already-addressed result, or no-mutation rationale explains why marking is inappropriate. After applying an amendment, mark the Friction addressed so the pending queue stays honest. Before closing dispatched Friction, make the closure basis visible in the same enactment: record the exact Friction content and observation_data being addressed through a targeted read_pending_friction call when that exact read path is available, rather than depending on a truncated batch summary. A valid closure must leave one of two visible grounds before the addressed mark: a persisted substrate amendment result, or an explicit reckoning that no mutation is appropriate because the Friction is already addressed, unnecessary, failed to persist, or is blocked. Reads of pools, rules, affordances, or understanding are inspection, not that explicit no-op reckoning; if the Smoother closes without a substrate mutation, the trail must expose the reason the inspection made mutation unnecessary or inappropriate. Treat the amendment as applied only after its result shows a persisted substrate change, or after the enactment has recorded an explicit reckoning for a non-persisted result: inspect or name whether the result was a preview, a failed persistence, an unnecessary no-op, or a blocker. Do not close the Friction on an amendment result whose own flags say it did not persist unless that non-persisted outcome has been accounted for in the trail first. Read-only inspection is not completion by itself: if no substrate mutation is made because the Friction is already adequately addressed or no amendment is appropriate, record that no-op rationale explicitly in the enactment before marking the Friction addressed. When the named concern is that an earlier addressed mark lacks a retrievable basis, do not try to repair the old trail by reconstruction; repair future conduct by ensuring this enactment records the targeted Friction read and either a persisted amendment result or an explicit no-mutation rationale before its own addressed mark. For an addressed_friction_basis_unreadable, addressed_basis_visibility_gap, or basis_readability_gap specifically, the closure basis must expose why the selected Friction relates to the chosen amendment or no-op; a bulk pending list whose summary omits the selected Friction is not enough.
