---
id: rule_smoother_mark_when_done
name: Ground before marking; non-Friction routed items are blockers
---
If a Smoother enactment has tended a pending Friction by reading it, inspecting its target, or applying amendment work for it, do not treat that work as closure by itself: before the enactment closes, invoke mark_friction_addressed for that friction_id, unless a same-enactment explicit blocker, already-addressed result, or no-mutation rationale explains why marking is inappropriate. An enactment that reads a pending Friction and then closes with no persisted amendment, no explicit no-mutation/blocker rationale, and no mark_friction_addressed step is unfinished smoothing, even if the read itself succeeded. A successful targeted read_pending_friction call that returns an empty result is not self-explaining closure: before closing, record a same-enactment disposition naming whether the id was absent, no longer pending, already addressed elsewhere, or intentionally left untouched because no mutation or mark is appropriate. When corrected targeted read_pending_friction calls return empty after a failed material-name or argument-shape recovery, the correction only resolves the failed invocation; it does not resolve the empty work item, so the enactment must record the empty-read disposition as absent, no longer pending, already addressed elsewhere, intentionally untouched, or blocker before closing. That empty targeted-read disposition is a blocker/no-mutation outcome, not a Friction closure: do not invoke mark_friction_addressed for the absent or already-addressed id merely to carry the disposition, because a not-found/already-addressed mark result does not persist and therefore leaves a failed_closure_attempt rather than a completed Smoother closure. If a targeted read_pending_friction call returns a record whose kind is `no_finding` or otherwise not a Friction concern requiring Smoother interpretation, treat that as a routed-item category mismatch, not as a Friction ready to clear: record the mismatch as the no-mutation/blocker rationale and do not mark that returned non-Friction item addressed through mark_friction_addressed. After applying an amendment to an actual Friction item, mark the Friction addressed so the pending queue stays honest. Before closing dispatched Friction, make the closure basis visible in the same enactment: record the exact Friction content and observation_data being addressed through a targeted read_pending_friction call when that exact read path is available, rather than depending on a truncated batch summary. A valid closure must leave one of two visible grounds before the addressed mark: a persisted substrate amendment result, or an explicit reckoning that no mutation is appropriate because the Friction is already addressed, unnecessary, failed to persist, or is blocked. Reads of pools, rules, affordances, or understanding are inspection, not that explicit no-op reckoning; if the Smoother closes without a substrate mutation, the trail must expose the reason the inspection made mutation unnecessary or inappropriate. Treat the amendment as applied only after its result shows a persisted substrate change, or after the enactment has recorded an explicit reckoning for a non-persisted result: inspect or name whether the result was a preview, a failed persistence, an unnecessary no-op, or a blocker. Do not close the Friction on an amendment result whose own flags say it did not persist unless that non-persisted outcome has been accounted for in the trail first. A failed targeted read_pending_friction call for the dispatched friction_id is itself a blocker to marking that Friction addressed: record the blocker and stop, unless a later successful targeted read of the same friction_id appears in this same enactment before any amendment, reload, substrate action, or addressed mark. When every targeted read attempt for the dispatched friction_id in an enactment has errored, that enactment has no exact-Friction closure basis; do not mark that Friction addressed, and do not treat a reload, pool read, bulk pending list, or successful amendment as a substitute for a later successful targeted read of the same id. If this enactment amends a Smoother rule that governs whether closure may proceed, the amended content governs the remaining steps of the same enactment: before mark_friction_addressed, check whether the just-persisted rule would now require stopping, blocking, or a different grounding step, and obey that current rule rather than proceeding under the pre-amendment habit. Read-only inspection is not completion by itself: if no substrate mutation is made because the Friction is already adequately addressed or no amendment is appropriate, record that no-op rationale explicitly in the enactment before marking the Friction addressed. When the named concern is an unread_friction_marked_addressed, ungrounded_friction_closure, addressed_friction_basis_unreadable, empty_pending_friction_lifecycle_gap, ungrounded_empty_friction_read, failed_closure_attempt, or another gap in an earlier addressed mark, failed addressed mark, or empty targeted read, do not try to repair the old trail by reconstruction; repair future conduct by ensuring this enactment records the targeted Friction read and either a persisted amendment result or an explicit no-mutation/blocker rationale before its own addressed mark. For an addressed_friction_basis_unreadable, addressed_basis_visibility_gap, basis_readability_gap, addressed_mark_basis_gap, unverified_addressed_basis, or similar closure-basis concern, the closure basis must expose why the selected Friction relates to the chosen amendment or no-op; a bulk pending list whose summary omits the selected Friction is not enough. If the visible amendment changes only a name or salience field while the Friction named missing behavior, missing content, material behavior, affordance shape, or bundle selection, do not mark addressed unless the trail first records why that name-only amendment is sufficient for the named concern; otherwise amend the behavioral content/surface the Friction names, or record an explicit no-mutation/blocker rationale instead. When the named concern is amendment_content_not_visible or another content-visibility gap, the Smoother's closure basis must make the substantive amendment prose visible in this enactment: pass the amended content, description, implementation, or selection field that addresses the Friction in the amendment arguments, or record an explicit no-mutation rationale explaining why no substantive content change is appropriate before marking addressed.

When a Smoother closure rationale asserts that an existing substrate surface already carries the requirement, material guidance, or behavioral content needed for one of the Friction's named targets, inspect that exact current surface in the same enactment before marking addressed. Use the available read that exposes the relied-on surface, such as the affordance, material, pool element, bundle selection, or governed invariant surface, and make the inspected id plus the relied-on prose or field visible in the trail or in the addressed rationale. If a rationale relies on current governed invariants by id, the trail must expose the invariant content, predicate, trigger, message, or other cited field before those invariants can supply an already-addressed/no-mutation basis. If no afforded read exposes the relied-on surface, record a basis-unreadable blocker or apply a substantive amendment that makes the requirement visible; do not close from an unsupported assertion that the substrate already covered it.

For addressed_without_grounded_amendment specifically, treat the missing ground as the named concern: before marking addressed, the same enactment must either apply a persisted amendment whose arguments expose the substantive content or surface change, or record that no mutation is needed because current substrate already contains the behavioral requirement and identify the rule or material text that supplies it.

For addressed_marker_outpaces_visible_amendment specifically, treat a trail-visible amendment that names only the pool, target id, or title as insufficient when the addressed Friction depends on a content, description, implementation, or selection change. Before marking addressed, this enactment must either apply a persisted amendment whose arguments expose the substantive changed prose or surface field, or record an explicit no-mutation/blocker rationale explaining why no such substantive amendment is appropriate. A non-persisted material amendment, preview, or failed persistence cannot be the closing amendment unless the trail accounts for that result and another persisted amendment or no-mutation rationale supplies the closure basis.
