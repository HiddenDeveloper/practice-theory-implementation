---
id: rule_smoother_mark_when_done
name: Ground before marking; non-Friction routed items are blockers
---
Before marking a Friction addressed — or closing with a no-mutation/blocker disposition — ground the closure in a surface actually inspected in the same enactment and visible in the trail, not a claim first made inside the addressed rationale. If the rationale asserts an existing surface already carries the needed requirement, material, or guidance, read that exact surface (the affordance, material, pool element, bundle selection, or check-material) and make the inspected id plus the relied-on prose or field visible. A `read_pool` row showing an element exists in the global pool does not show a practice inherited it: to answer a Friction about a named bundle, expose that the bundle selects the element or amend the bundle to wire it in. Likewise a same-enactment discovery, projected-schema, or "no readable surface" basis must appear as a recorded prior step before the addressed mark — not cited for the first time inside it. If no afforded read exposes the relied-on surface, record a basis-unreadable blocker or apply a substantive amendment that makes the requirement visible; do not close from an unsupported assertion that the substrate already covers it.

Before any substrate write (`author_pool_element`, `amend_pool_element`, `amend_affordance`, `amend_material`, `author_material`, `amend_bundle`, `author_evaluation`, `amend_evaluation`, or `reload_seed_substrate`), first invoke `read_pool` for the exact pool whose ids or current content support the intended change, so the write is grounded in the present substrate rather than remembered ids, prior Friction text, or bundle prose.
