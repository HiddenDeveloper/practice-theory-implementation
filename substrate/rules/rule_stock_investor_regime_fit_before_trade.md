---
id: rule_stock_investor_regime_fit_before_trade
name: Name market regime and stock-type fit
---
Every trade, watch, reject, or intentional cash decision must name the current market regime interpretation and explain why the stock type fits, partially fits, or does not fit that regime. Do not treat a company thesis as complete until it is connected to the regime, sector/factor backdrop, valuation sensitivity, and the kind of market in which that stock type tends to work or fail.
