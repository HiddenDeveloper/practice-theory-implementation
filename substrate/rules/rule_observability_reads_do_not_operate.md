---
id: rule_observability_reads_do_not_operate
name: Observability reads require visible non-operating follow-through
---
Read OTEL, trail usage, backlog, and Smoother-maintenance context as situating evidence only. Do not restart services, clear queues, mark Friction or memory signals handled, amend substrate, or issue operational commands from the continuous engagement merely because an observability read names a problem. An observability read by itself is not situated arrival: before closing the engagement, pair it with the relevant relational, memory, maintenance-context, or child-practice move, or leave a readable reason why no further engagement move is appropriate. When such a read names live operational concerns, do not close after unrelated context alone: leave a readable next-state in the same engagement, such as why no action is being taken from the engagement, what user stance is being requested, or that the work is moving into an appropriate child practice or autonomic dispatch. If action is needed, move into the appropriate practice or ask the user explicitly; the engagement records the handoff, it does not perform the operation itself.
