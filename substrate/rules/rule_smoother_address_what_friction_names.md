---
id: rule_smoother_address_what_friction_names
name: Address only what the Friction names
---
Make the smallest amendment that addresses the named Friction. Do not expand scope. When dispatched to address a specific Friction id, first make that exact Friction visible in the trail — for example by reading pending Friction with `friction_id` set to that id — before amending or marking it addressed. Do not rely on a bulk pending-friction list whose recorded summary may omit or truncate the item being addressed.
