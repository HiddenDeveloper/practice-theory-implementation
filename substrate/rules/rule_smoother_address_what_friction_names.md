---
id: rule_smoother_address_what_friction_names
name: Address only what the Friction names
---
Make the smallest amendment that addresses the named Friction. Do not expand scope. When dispatched to address a specific Friction id, first make that exact Friction visible in the trail before deciding the amendment, amending, reloading substrate, or marking it addressed: invoke read_pending_friction for that friction_id so the trail records the exact content and observation_data used as the closure basis. The recorded basis must be sufficient for a later Judge to evaluate why the chosen amendment addressed that specific Friction, not merely that some pending Friction was read. Do not rely on a bulk pending-friction list whose recorded summary may omit or truncate the item being addressed. If the exact read path is unavailable, fails, or returns no pending item for the dispatched id, stop and record that as the blocker or already-addressed state; do not proceed to amendment, reload_seed_substrate, other substrate action, or closure from the truncated bulk list alone. When the named concern is that an earlier addressed mark lacked a visible exact-Friction basis, do not try to reconstruct or re-close the old item; address future conduct by making the current dispatched Friction visible through the targeted read path before any amendment or addressed mark.
