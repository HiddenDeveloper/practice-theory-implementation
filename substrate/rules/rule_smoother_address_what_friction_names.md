---
id: rule_smoother_address_what_friction_names
name: Address only what Friction names; failed targeted reads stop work
---
Make the smallest amendment that addresses the named Friction. Do not expand scope. When dispatched to address a specific Friction id, first make that exact Friction visible in the trail before deciding the amendment, amending, reloading substrate, or marking it addressed: invoke read_pending_friction for that friction_id so the trail records the exact content and observation_data used as the closure basis. The recorded basis must be sufficient for a later Judge to evaluate why the chosen amendment addressed that specific Friction, not merely that some pending Friction was read. A description or amendment saying targeted reads are required is not itself a closure basis; the Smoother must actually invoke read_pending_friction with the dispatched friction_id in the same enactment before mark_friction_addressed. Do not rely on a bulk pending-friction list whose recorded summary may omit or truncate the item being addressed. If the exact read path is unavailable, fails, or returns no pending item for the dispatched id, stop and record that as the blocker or already-addressed state; do not proceed to amendment, reload_seed_substrate, other substrate action, or closure from the truncated bulk list alone. When the named concern is that an earlier addressed mark lacked a visible exact-Friction basis, do not try to reconstruct or re-close the old item; address future conduct by making the current dispatched Friction visible through the targeted read path before any amendment or addressed mark.

Friction 1042 confirms that a successful targeted Friction read is only the opening basis, not a completed Smoother response. After the exact pending Friction has been read, the next Smoother work must interpret what it names and produce one of the projected resolution shapes: a grounded substrate amendment, a grounded no-mutation/already-addressed/blocker disposition, or another required recovery/accounting row before those actions when an invocation failed. Do not close a Smoother enactment after read_pending_friction alone, even after a corrected retry; if there is a closure basis, mark_friction_addressed after it is visible, and if there is no closure basis, record the concrete blocker rather than silently stopping.

Friction 1126 confirms the same completion requirement applies after the Smoother has performed additional inspection, such as `read_pool`, following the targeted Friction read. A read of pending Friction plus pool or bundle context is still only an opened response unless the trail then shows a resolution shape. After inspection has supplied the decision basis, the Smoother must either apply the smallest grounded substrate amendment and then mark the dispatched Friction addressed, mark it addressed from a visibly grounded no-mutation/already-addressed basis when appropriate, or record a concrete blocker/no-mutation disposition and stop when closure is not available. Do not close silently after read-only inspection, and do not treat the fact that a relevant rule or pool was read as the completion disposition itself.
