---
id: rule_stock_investor_live_market_snapshot
name: Fetch live market evidence before allocation
---
Before recording a buy, sell, trim, add, reject, watch, material hold, blocker-state hold/disposition, or all-cash posture decision, invoke the live market snapshot material through the market-reading affordance. Use its returned prices, timestamps, source URLs, and limitations as the evidence basis. If the live read fails or is incomplete, record that failure as a measurement gap and make only decisions that are justified by the remaining evidence. Friction 801 confirms this gate applies to hold decisions recorded during stock-investor reviews: after fund state and follow-up reads, do not proceed to record_trade_decision for a hold unless the same pass has reached market_fetch_snapshot, or has explicitly recorded the live-read failure/incompleteness as the measurement gap constraining that hold. Friction 819 confirms the same gate also applies when the pass frames the result as a blocker-state hold or disposition rather than an ordinary allocation review: after reading fund state, the next decision/disposition path must first reach market_fetch_snapshot, or visibly record the snapshot failure/incompleteness as the live-market measurement gap constraining the blocker-state outcome.

Friction 823 confirms the remaining bypass is a follow-up-register blocker-state path: after fund state and current follow-up reads, recording a hold, blocker-state disposition, decision report, or follow-up-register update that decides to continue holding, stay in cash, defer construction, or leave exposure unchanged is still a decision/disposition for this rule. The next visible stock-investor move before that record must be `read_live_market_snapshot` / `market_fetch_snapshot`, or a concrete live-market snapshot failure/incompleteness recorded as the measurement gap constraining the blocker-state outcome. Do not treat the absence of a trade, or a register-only blocker update, as permission to skip the live market snapshot.

Friction 832 confirms this is an affordance-coverage quality concern rather than a wording preference: evaluated stock-investor passes read fund state and then recorded blocker-state decisions or follow-up dispositions without a same-enactment `market_fetch_snapshot`. For future stock-investor passes, a fund-state read that leads to any decision, blocker-state disposition, hold/no-action posture, cash-construction deferral, or follow-up-register update preserving current exposure must be paired in that same enactment with `read_live_market_snapshot` / `market_fetch_snapshot` before the decision or disposition is recorded. If the snapshot is deliberately not reopened because same-day evidence seems known, the pass must instead record a concrete live-market snapshot blocker or incompleteness as the constraint; prior-day, earlier-enactment, remembered, or not-reopened market context is not the required coverage row.

Friction 840 confirms the same gate still fails after the stock-investor pass has read both fund state and the follow-up register: a blocker-state or hold disposition that preserves exposure, leaves construction deferred, updates follow-ups, or records a no-action outcome is not grounded until the same enactment has produced `read_live_market_snapshot` / `market_fetch_snapshot`. After `fund_read_state` plus `fund_read_follow_up_register`, the immediate next decision-bearing row must be the live market snapshot, or a concrete live-snapshot failure/incompleteness recorded as the measurement gap; do not proceed to `fund_record_trade_decision`, decision-report writing, or follow-up-register updates that preserve the posture from state/follow-up context alone.

Friction 844 confirms the anti-repetition/blocker-state path is still a live bypass: reading fund state and follow-ups, then deciding not to reopen the same-day market evidence because the pass is only recording blocker-state handling, still fails `reads_state_and_market_before_deciding`. Anti-repetition may shorten the review, but it must not remove the coverage row. After fund state is read, any hold/no-action disposition, blocker-state note, report, or follow-up-register update that preserves exposure must either first reach `read_live_market_snapshot` / `market_fetch_snapshot` in the same enactment or record a concrete live-snapshot failure/incompleteness as the measurement gap constraining that limited disposition.
