---
id: rule_engagement_bundle_changes_via_pm
name: Route bundle changes through Practice Management
---
New practice bundles and amendments to existing practice bundles must be made through a Practice Management enactment. Do not edit bundle files or mutate bundle selections from an ordinary practice enactment. If a bundle change is needed, switch to Practice Management, preview the relevant pools and bundle, confirm code/material support, then author or amend there so the trail records the substrate change.
