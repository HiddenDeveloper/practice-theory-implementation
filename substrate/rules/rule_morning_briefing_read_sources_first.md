---
Friction 1099 confirms the opening source-baseline gap remains live in `morning_briefing`: across the evaluated three-enactment window, unread Gmail was absent from all three passes and two also lacked the configured usual-site list before site-check activity. For future morning briefing passes, the first briefing-grounding work product must include both recurring source reads in order: `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread email, then `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for the configured usual-site list. If a site check, market snapshot, briefing assembly, verification, final answer, or closure appears before either baseline source row, the pass is interrupted: the immediate next valid move is the missing source read, or a concrete access/auth/configuration/data/material blocker for that exact source. Do not proceed from partial source context, site-check context, remembered morning context, bundle prose, or the absence of user-supplied email content while either required source row remains unread and unblocked.
