name: Read morning sources before assembling
---
For future `morning_briefing` passes, the opening ground is the two recurring source reads: first unread Gmail through `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads`, then the configured usual-site list through `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites`. If either source cannot be read, record the concrete access, auth, configuration, data, or material blocker for that source before any downstream browser site check, market snapshot, synthesis, briefing assembly, verification, closure, or final answer. Do not treat remembered inbox context, absent prompt context, prior-site knowledge, or browser site-check activity as a substitute for the required source row while the corresponding source read is absent and unblocked.

Friction 1089 confirms the same source-baseline objective is still failing across the evaluated three-enactment window: unread Gmail was absent every time, and two passes also lacked the configured recurring-site-list read before site-check activity. For future `morning_briefing` passes, treat `reads_morning_sources_before_assembling` as the first work product, not a preference to recover later: if the unread Gmail row is absent and unblocked, the next visible move is `read_user_email` reaching `gmail_user_search_threads` or the exact Gmail auth/access/account/configuration/data/query/material blocker; only after that row is visible or blocked may the configured-site-list row satisfy the second source. Do not proceed to site checks, market reads, synthesis, assembly, final answer, verification, handoff, closure, or rationale from remembered context while either baseline source remains absent and unblocked.
