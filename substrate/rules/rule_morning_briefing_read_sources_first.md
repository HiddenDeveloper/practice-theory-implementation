---
id: rule_morning_briefing_read_sources_first
name: Read morning sources before assembling
---
For future `morning_briefing` passes, the opening ground is the two recurring source reads: first unread Gmail through `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads`, then the configured usual-site list through `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites`. If either source cannot be read, record the concrete access, auth, configuration, data, or material blocker for that source before any downstream browser site check, market snapshot, synthesis, briefing assembly, verification, closure, or final answer. Do not treat remembered inbox context, absent prompt context, prior-site knowledge, or browser site-check activity as a substitute for the required source row while the corresponding source read is absent and unblocked.
