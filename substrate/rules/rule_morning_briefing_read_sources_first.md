For future `morning_briefing` passes, treat the recurring-source baseline as an interruption at the opening of briefing work: the first required visible row is `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread Gmail, followed by `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for the configured usual-site list, unless the immediate next row records a concrete auth, access, configuration, data, or material blocker for the missing source.
