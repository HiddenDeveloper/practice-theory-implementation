---
id: rule_activities_read_record_before_rhythm
name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 737 confirms this remains a genuine `activities_management` quality concern after the Friction 730 sharpening: recent passes still used adjacent Garmin detail/daily-summary surfaces or stale material attempts without first grounding the current activity list. For `activities_management`, make `recent_activity` reaching `garmin_list_activities` the first visible activity-grounding move in any broad or ambiguous request about recent activity context, completed activity records, what was done, activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, training rhythm, weekly pattern, or follow-on deepening from activity detail/daily summary. Do this before `activity_detail` / `garmin_get_activity`, `daily_summary` / `garmin_get_daily_summary`, route, GPS-shape, IWT-analysis, visualization, synthesis, or any recovery from a stale/misnamed Garmin material attempt. The only exceptions are an explicitly isolated supplied activity id/date, one explicitly bounded daily wellness snapshot, or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker that is named and limits the answer. If any adjacent Garmin read or failed/stale Garmin material attempt happens before the list and the work will say anything broader than that isolated record or snapshot, the immediate next visible move must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` or an explicit blocker/limit; do not continue to more context gathering, synthesis, stance, or final answer while the current activity list is absent.

Friction 744 confirms the remaining miss pattern: practitioners still entered through single activity detail, single daily summary, or stale Garmin material failures and then treated that isolated surface as enough for rhythm or "what was done" work. A direct detail read, direct daily-summary read, stale material error, or material-name recovery is not a substitute for the current activity list. Unless the user explicitly asks only for that one supplied activity id/date or one bounded daily wellness snapshot and the answer stays inside that isolated scope, the next visible move after any such direct or failed adjacent Garmin step must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` before describing recent activity rhythm, activity mix, completed activity records, gaps, streaks, cadence, weekly pattern, or follow-on analysis. If the list cannot be reached, name that exact list/auth/data/material blocker and keep the answer limited to the isolated record or snapshot.
