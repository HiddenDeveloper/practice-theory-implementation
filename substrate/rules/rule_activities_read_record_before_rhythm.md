id: rule_activities_read_record_before_rhythm
name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 798 confirms that the failure persists specifically across adjacent Garmin surfaces: four of five recent `activities_management` passes reached `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, or stale Garmin material attempts without first invoking `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`. Treat any such adjacent Garmin row before the list read as positive evidence that the activity-rhythm pass has not yet begun from the current activity record. The correction is not to continue interpreting the adjacent result narrowly; the immediate next move must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker, before any description of what was done, rhythm, completed-record mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, recovery context, route/GPS/IWT context, visualization, or final answer.

Friction 802 confirms that the same gate still fails in a later evaluated window even though the bundle description names `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` as the entry grounding move. For future `activities_management` passes, the activity-record list is not optional context and not merely a recommended preface: when the pass may describe activity rhythm, daily activity, completed-record mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, route/GPS/IWT context, visualization, or an activity-detail finding, the next visible practice move after opening must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, unless the practitioner first records a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker or explicitly confines the pass to a named isolated limit that does not synthesize rhythm or activity-record state. If any adjacent Garmin surface or stale Garmin material attempt appears before the list read, treat the pass as interrupted; the immediate recovery move is `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` or the concrete blocker, and no synthesis, final answer, or closure should occur until that grounding row is visible.
