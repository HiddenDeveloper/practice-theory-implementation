id: rule_activities_read_record_before_rhythm
name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 1003 confirms the remaining `activities_management` quality gap is not just ordinary detail-before-list drift: evaluated passes also entered stale or unavailable activity detail and daily-summary material paths without first grounding in `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`. Keep the existing entry gate, and treat a stale material name, unavailable reached material, wrong detail/daily-summary surface, or adjacent Garmin recovery attempt as an interrupted activity-rhythm path whenever the work may describe what was done, daily rhythm, week pattern, activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, route/GPS/IWT context, or visualization context. The next visible move after such a stale/detail/daily-summary attempt must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker; do not continue to another activity detail, daily summary, synthesis, final answer, or closure from the stale-material error alone. The only non-list exception remains language explicitly confined to one supplied isolated activity id/date or one daily wellness snapshot with no activity-record or rhythm characterization.
