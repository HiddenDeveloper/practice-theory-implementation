Friction 1026 confirms the same `activities_management` practice-quality coverage concern remains after the rule and bundle description already name `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` as the entry grounding move: four of five evaluated enactments still reached `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, or stale adjacent Garmin material attempts without the list row, and the parent scope reads did not expose an isolated-activity or daily-snapshot exception. For future `activities_management` passes, a direct `garmin_get_activity`, `garmin_get_daily_summary`, stale detail/summary alias, route/GPS/IWT/visualization read, or adjacent activity-rhythm surface is an ungrounded start unless the same enactment has already listed recent activities. If such an adjacent row appears first, the pass is interrupted: the immediate next visible activity-management move must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker that explicitly confines the response to the supplied isolated activity id, date, or already-visible daily wellness evidence. Do not synthesize rhythm, describe what was done, answer from detail or daily-summary context, verify, close, or move to another adjacent Garmin surface while the list row is absent and unblocked.

Friction 1035 confirms this remains a genuine `activities_management` practice-quality coverage stall: four of five evaluated enactments still skipped `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` before activity detail, daily summary, or stale adjacent Garmin material attempts, despite a nearby passing trace showing the list path is reachable. Treat the first adjacent Garmin read or stale-material attempt without the list row as the interruption point, not as a recoverable context source. After `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, route, GPS-shape, IWT-analysis, visualization, or any stale detail/summary alias appears before `garmin_list_activities`, the only permitted continuation is `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` itself or a concrete blocker naming the exact list/auth/data/material condition and explicitly limiting any response to already-supplied isolated activity/date/evidence. Do not answer from the detail, summary, alias-error, parent-scope, or remembered activity context; do not verify, close, or move to another adjacent Garmin surface while the current activity list remains absent and unblocked.
