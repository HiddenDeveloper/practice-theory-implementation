name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 1013 confirms the `activities_management` coverage stall remains genuine even after the bundle description names `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` as the entry grounding move: four of five evaluated recent enactments reached `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, stale adjacent Garmin material attempts, or activity-rhythm-adjacent work without a same-enactment list row, while nearby evidence showed the list path was reachable. Keep this as a first-work-product rule. For any broad or ambiguous activity/rhythm, what-was-done, week-pattern, activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, route, GPS-shape, IWT, or visualization path, the opening Garmin move must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`. If an enactment has already touched `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, a stale Garmin alias, route/GPS/IWT/visualization, or another adjacent Garmin surface first, it is interrupted: the immediate next visible move must be `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker that explicitly confines the answer to the isolated id/date or daily wellness evidence already read. Do not continue to another adjacent Garmin read, synthesis, final answer, verification, or closure from detail/daily-summary context alone.

Friction 1019 confirms the remaining failure is not lack of description but failure to treat the list row as the first recoverable activity record. In `activities_management`, `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, stale material names such as `garmin_get_activity_detail` or `garmin_daily_summary`, and adjacent route/GPS/IWT/visualization reads do not establish the activity/rhythm record by themselves when the parent engagement has no visible limiting context. After any such first move, the pass is still ungrounded until it either invokes `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` in the same enactment, or records a concrete blocker naming why the activity list cannot be reached and explicitly limits any answer to the supplied isolated activity id, date, or already-visible daily wellness evidence. Do not treat a stale-material error as harmless unless the corrected next move is the projected list material or the blocker is recorded before further detail, summary, synthesis, final answer, or closure.
