---
id: rule_activities_read_record_before_rhythm
name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 774 confirms the remaining miss is not absence of the list gate but misclassification of adjacent Garmin starts as acceptable isolated-snapshot work. In `activities_management`, a supplied date or successful daily-summary/detail lookup does not by itself create the exception. A request phrased as what was done, recent activity, activity rhythm, weekly pattern, completed records, mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, recovery context, route/GPS/IWT, or visualization remains an activity-record request even when it names a single day; the first or immediate recovery move must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` unless the practitioner explicitly states that the answer is limited only to one supplied activity id/date or one bounded daily wellness snapshot. Stale adjacent material errors such as `garmin_get_activity_detail` or `garmin_daily_summary` are also unfinished activity-record grounding: after such an error, recover to the projected `garmin_list_activities` surface before synthesis or final answer, or record the concrete list/auth/data/material blocker and keep the answer inside the isolated evidence.

Friction 780 confirms the drift persists even when nearby passing traces show `garmin_list_activities` is reachable. Treat a missing list read before `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, route, GPS-shape, IWT-analysis, visualization, or stale adjacent material recovery as an interrupted pass, not as acceptable variation. Once any adjacent Garmin surface has appeared before `garmin_list_activities`, the next visible move must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker; do not continue to another Garmin read, activity-rhythm synthesis, completed-record summary, gaps/streaks/cadence claim, or final answer from detail-only or daily-summary-only evidence.

Friction 783 confirms this is still a genuine `activities_management` practice-quality concern across another recent window: four of five evaluated enactments used activity detail, daily summary, or stale adjacent Garmin material without the `garmin_list_activities` entry row, while a nearby passing trace showed the list read was reachable. Therefore the list read is the first visible work product for any activity/rhythm or what-was-done pass, not guidance to satisfy later if convenient. If `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, route, GPS-shape, IWT-analysis, visualization, or a stale Garmin alias appears before `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, the pass is interrupted; the immediate next move must be that list read or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker, and no activity-rhythm summary, completed-record mix, gaps/streaks/cadence claim, or final answer should proceed from adjacent Garmin evidence alone.
