---
id: rule_activities_read_record_before_rhythm
name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 824 confirms the activity-list gate still fails in the exact shape the rule is meant to prevent: recent `activities_management` enactments used `garmin_get_activity`, stale `garmin_get_activity_detail`, `garmin_get_daily_summary`, or stale `garmin_daily_summary` before `garmin_list_activities`, and then treated those adjacent rows as enough basis for an activity/rhythm pass. For future passes, make `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` the first Garmin-affordance row whenever the pass could touch what was done, daily activity, completed-activity mix, rhythm, gaps, streaks, cadence, route/GPS/IWT context, visualization, or an activity-detail finding. A pre-list direct detail, daily-summary, route/GPS/IWT/visualization call, or stale-material error is not partial grounding and is not a permitted entry path; it is an interrupted pass whose only next visible move is `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete blocker/isolated-limit statement that explicitly forbids rhythm or activity-record synthesis until the list row exists.

Friction 825 confirms this is still a live practice-quality failure rather than acceptable variation: four of five rerun-evaluated `activities_management` enactments reached adjacent Garmin detail or daily-summary surfaces without `garmin_list_activities`, while a nearby control showed the list path is reachable. Treat the absence of `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` before any adjacent Garmin detail, daily summary, route, GPS-shape, IWT-analysis, visualization, or broad activity-rhythm synthesis as an immediate stop condition at the start of the pass. The only permitted continuation from that state is the exact `garmin_list_activities` row, or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker or isolated-record limit that explicitly confines the answer away from rhythm, weekly pattern, completed-activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, and what-was-done claims. Do not proceed from adjacent Garmin context, stale-material recovery, bundle description, or remembered prior activity knowledge while the current-enactment list row remains absent and unblocked.
