Friction 1054 confirms the remaining `activities_management` quality gap is not lack of wording but failure to make the required list row the first usable activity-management work product: four of five evaluated enactments reached `garmin_get_activity`, `garmin_get_daily_summary`, or stale adjacent Garmin material attempts without `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, while a nearby contrast pass showed the list surface was available. For future `activities_management` passes, a single activity detail, daily summary, route/GPS/IWT/visualization row, or stale detail/summary material error is not a grounding substitute for the current activity list. If any of those adjacent rows appears before `garmin_list_activities`, the pass is interrupted and the next visible activity-management move must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker that confines any response to an explicitly supplied isolated activity id, date, or already-visible daily wellness datum. Do not synthesize what was done, rhythm, week pattern, completed-activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, verification, final answer, or closure from detail, summary, stale-alias, parent-scope, remembered, or bundle-description context while the current activity-list row is absent and unblocked.
