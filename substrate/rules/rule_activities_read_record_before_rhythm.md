name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 1078 narrows the remaining `activities_management` miss to the exception path: parent engagement reads or supplied ids/dates do not by themselves establish the isolated-activity or daily-snapshot exception. If `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, a stale adjacent Garmin material attempt, route/GPS/IWT/visualization, or any activity-context synthesis appears before `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, the pass must make one of two bases visible before continuing: either the current list row, or an explicit response-side limiter naming the single supplied activity id/date or daily wellness snapshot plus the concrete list/auth/data/material reason the list row is unavailable or out of scope. A parent step returning no visible response-side limiting basis, remembered scope, bundle prose, or adjacent detail/summary result is not enough to hold the exception; without that visible limiter, the next move remains `garmin_list_activities` and no rhythm, week-pattern, completed-activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, verification, final answer, or closure should proceed.

Friction 1081 confirms the remaining bypass includes successful Pilates/activity detail, daily summary, and failed adjacent Garmin material attempts that are then allowed to stand in for current activity/rhythm grounding. For future `activities_management` passes, an `activity_detail` or `daily_summary` result only describes the named record or day; it is not by itself the visible isolated-scope limiter and it does not authorize rhythm, recent-activity, completed-activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, weekly-pattern, IWT, route/GPS-shape, visualization, verification, final answer, or closure work. After any adjacent detail/summary read or failed/stale Garmin material attempt that occurs before `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, the immediate next activity-rhythm move must be `garmin_list_activities`, unless the same response first records the explicit limiter and concrete list-unavailable or out-of-scope reason named above.

Friction 1088 confirms this remains a live affordance-coverage failure in the evaluated `activities_management` window: four of five recent passes still reached direct detail, direct daily-summary, or stale Garmin material paths without `garmin_list_activities`, while a nearby comparison pass showed the list row was reachable. Treat that pattern as loss of the observer/recorder surface, not acceptable variation. When any Garmin activity-adjacent row or stale material attempt appears before the current activity list, the pass is interrupted: the only valid continuation is `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker plus a response visibly confined to the isolated activity id/date or daily snapshot. Do not proceed to another adjacent read, synthesis, verification, final answer, or closure from stale-alias recovery, detail context, daily-summary context, remembered ids, or bundle prose while the list row remains unread and unblocked.
