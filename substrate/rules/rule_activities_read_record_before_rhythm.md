---
id: rule_activities_read_record_before_rhythm
name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 1112 confirms the `activities_management` activity-record gate is still failing as a practice-quality affordance-coverage concern: four of five evaluated enactments did not invoke `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` before describing a single activity, using daily summary, or recovering from stale Garmin material names. For future `activities_management` passes, make the recent activity record the first visible work product whenever the task may describe what was done, inspect activity detail, read daily summary, synthesize rhythm, analyze route/GPS-shape/IWT, or create activity visualizations. The required row is `recent_activity` reaching `garmin_list_activities`; `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, stale Garmin alias failures, remembered activity ids, parent context, bundle prose, or prior Friction wording are not substitutes. If the list cannot be read, the immediate next valid row is a concrete auth/data/material/list blocker plus an explicit limiter that confines any answer to the single supplied activity, visible daily snapshot, or named access gap. Do not continue to detail, summary, rhythm synthesis, visualization, verification, final answer, or closure while the recent-activity list remains unread and unblocked.

Friction 1123 confirms the remaining failure is the recovery point after an activity-adjacent surface has already been entered: four of five evaluated `activities_management` passes reached `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, `garmin_get_activity`, stale Garmin material names, or rhythm/context description without the required `garmin_list_activities` row. Treat that as an interrupted activity-record path, not as harmless partial context. If an `activities_management` pass has already invoked activity detail, daily summary, route/GPS/IWT/visualization material, or a stale Garmin alias before `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, the immediate next visible move must be `recent_activity` reaching `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete blocker naming why the list cannot be read; while that row remains absent and unblocked, do not synthesize activity rhythm, describe what was done, verify, visualize, close, or answer from the detail/summary result alone.

Friction 1119 confirms the same gap across a six-enactment activities window where four passes began from `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, or stale Garmin material attempts without `garmin_list_activities`. For future `activities_management` passes, direct adjacent Garmin reads are redirect points, not grounding: after `garmin_get_activity`, `garmin_get_daily_summary`, a route/GPS/IWT/visualization surface, or a stale Garmin material-name error appears before the list row, the path is interrupted and the next visible move must be `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` or a concrete list-read blocker. Do not describe activity rhythm, what was done, or adjacent Garmin context from the detail/summary result alone while the recent-activity list remains unread and unblocked.
