name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 1078 narrows the remaining `activities_management` miss to the exception path: parent engagement reads or supplied ids/dates do not by themselves establish the isolated-activity or daily-snapshot exception. If `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, a stale adjacent Garmin material attempt, route/GPS/IWT/visualization, or any activity-context synthesis appears before `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, the pass must make one of two bases visible before continuing: either the current list row, or an explicit response-side limiter naming the single supplied activity id/date or daily wellness snapshot plus the concrete list/auth/data/material reason the list row is unavailable or out of scope. A parent step returning no visible response-side limiting basis, remembered scope, bundle prose, or adjacent detail/summary result is not enough to hold the exception; without that visible limiter, the next move remains `garmin_list_activities` and no rhythm, week-pattern, completed-activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, verification, final answer, or closure should proceed.
