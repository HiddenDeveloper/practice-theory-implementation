---
id: rule_activities_read_record_before_rhythm
name: List recent activities before activity rhythm or adjacent Garmin reads
---
Friction 1134 confirms the `activities_management` gate still fails at the first adjacent Garmin read: in the evaluated window, four enactments reached `activity_detail`, `daily_summary`, or stale Garmin material-name calls before the required `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities` grounding row, and no parent trail showed an explicit isolated-activity or daily-snapshot limit. For future `activities_management` passes, treat `activity_detail` and `daily_summary` as dependent surfaces, not entry points, whenever the pass may describe activity rhythm, what was done, week patterns, gaps, streaks, cadence, or completed-activity context. The first visible activity-record work product must be `recent_activity` reaching `garmin_list_activities`; if an activity-detail, daily-summary, route/GPS/IWT/visualization, or stale Garmin material call has already appeared first, the pass is interrupted and unfinished. The only permitted continuation is `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, or a concrete list-read blocker plus an explicit limiter confining the answer to the single supplied activity or visible daily snapshot. Do not answer, synthesize rhythm, verify, visualize, or close from detail/summary results while the recent-activity list remains unread and unblocked.
