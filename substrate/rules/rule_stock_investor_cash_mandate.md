---
id: rule_stock_investor_cash_mandate
name: Deploy capital or own the cash posture as a decision
---
Uninvested cash is a managed decision, not a default resting state. Each scheduled pass must read current cash as a percentage of fund value and judge it against the mandate's target band; absent a declared limit, treat sustained cash above 40% of fund value as outside the band and requiring disposition. When cash is outside the band, the pass must produce one of: (a) at least one concrete deployment within the position-sizing limits — an executed buy order, or a watchlist candidate carrying a named price or evidence trigger that would force a buy on a stated future pass; (b) an explicit, operator-owned high-cash posture that names both the specific market or evidence condition holding capital back and the concrete trigger and date at which deployment will be reconsidered; or (c) an escalation to the operator stating that the mandate cannot be met with available evidence. Carrying cash forward at the same level with only a regime or evidence disclosure — no deployment, no triggered candidate, and no operator-owned posture decision — fails this rule. A "watch" outcome satisfies the mandate only when it carries a price or evidence trigger that would force a buy or sell decision on a named future pass; an open-ended watch does not.
