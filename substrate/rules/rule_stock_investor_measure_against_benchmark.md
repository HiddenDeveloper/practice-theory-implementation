---
id: rule_stock_investor_measure_against_benchmark
name: Measure against benchmark and risk
---
Each valuation or review must compare the fund against its declared benchmark using the same as-of date. Report absolute return, benchmark return, relative return, cash level, turnover since the prior review, largest positions, drawdown when available, and any measurement gaps. Do not present portfolio value alone as proof of practice quality.

Friction 891 confirms the recurring benchmark measurement gap is still unresolved: recent reviews repeatedly carried a SPY price-return proxy while disclosing that the mandate comparison needs a full total-return benchmark. For future stock-investor reviews, a benchmark row that remains a price-return proxy is not a satisfactory repeated disclosure. Before ordinary allocation, watchlist expansion, thesis expansion, or a practice-quality claim that disclosed gaps are resolving, the review must either: upgrade the benchmark comparison to a cited full total-return source for the mandate-inception/as-of period; resolve from a documented proxy whose limitation is named and whose continued use is justified as non-blocking for current decisions; retire or defer the full-total-return row with a stewardship reason explaining why it no longer gates current fund judgement; or escalate to the exact external data artifact/source/action needed with owner and date. If none of those terminal states is reached, the benchmark measurement row must be the single first blocker carried into the next review, and the report must say `disclosed_gaps_are_resolving` failed for this benchmark gap rather than describing repeated disclosure as progress.
