---
id: rule_judge_examine_before_naming
name: Discover and examine before naming
---
Read the enactment's steps and the bundle's affordances before deciding whether Friction applies. When judging a concrete target enactment, first invoke list_recent_enactments, filtered to the target's bundle when known, so the trail records the discovery window used to situate the target before the direct step read; if a known or dispatched target is absent from that window, preserve the list-versus-direct mismatch in observation_data rather than treating absence from the recent window as proof about the target's steps. Do not name Friction without evidence in the trail. If a Friction observation quotes, applies, or depends on substrate text such as a rule, understanding, material description, or affordance description, make that text visible through a recorded affordance step before using it as the observation's basis; a bundle read that returns only ids grounds the selected ids, not the selected elements' prose. If the needed text cannot be retrieved from the Judge seat, name the evidence-source/readability gap or carry the claim as local context rather than presenting the unseen text as trail-grounded evidence. When judging a session-scope holder such as continuous_self, preserve direct parent steps separately from work enacted under child practices even when the holder has some direct steps. A bundle-filtered list for the holder is not a child-practice visibility check. Before naming narrow_engagement on the holder, inspect whether child enactments are visible through the trail, for example via recent enactments that carry parent_enactment_id; if child enactments are visible and the comparison depends on what work happened under the holder, read those child steps or record why they cannot be read. If child visibility cannot be grounded, name that visibility/readability gap rather than treating the holder's direct step mix as the whole session. When an enactment has zero recorded steps, do not collapse that fact into abandonment if it may be an engagement-scope parent holder: first check whether child practice enactments are visible from the trail. If child visibility is unavailable, name the empty-trail/readability ambiguity itself and preserve the benign holder reading beside the concerning reading. If the target bundle or its affordance set is not readable from the Judge seat -- for example because the enactment names a somatic engagement bundle outside the autonomic catalog -- do not complete an available-vs-used comparison from the readable half alone. Name the missing denominator or mode-boundary visibility gap as the Friction, carrying the readable used-side evidence beside the failed bundle read, and leave any narrow/full-engagement judgement unresolved. In particular, do not emit narrow_engagement for a somatic engagement solely because its engagement-layer step list is empty when the bundle side or child-practice visibility cannot be grounded; emit empty_trail or missing_bundle/readability Friction instead.
