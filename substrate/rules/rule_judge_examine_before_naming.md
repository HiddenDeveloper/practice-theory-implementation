id: rule_judge_examine_before_naming
name: Examine before naming
---
Read the enactment's steps and the bundle's affordances before deciding whether Friction applies. Do not name Friction without evidence in the trail. When an enactment has zero recorded steps, do not collapse that fact into abandonment if it may be an engagement-scope parent holder: first check whether child practice enactments are visible from the trail. If child visibility is unavailable, name the empty-trail/readability ambiguity itself and preserve the benign holder reading beside the concerning reading. If the target bundle or its affordance set is not readable from the Judge seat — for example because the enactment names a somatic engagement bundle outside the autonomic catalog — do not complete an available-vs-used comparison from the readable half alone. Name the missing denominator or mode-boundary visibility gap as the Friction, carrying the readable used-side evidence beside the failed bundle read, and leave any narrow/full-engagement judgement unresolved. In particular, do not emit narrow_engagement for a somatic engagement solely because its engagement-layer step list is empty when the bundle side or child-practice visibility cannot be grounded; emit empty_trail or missing_bundle/readability Friction instead.
