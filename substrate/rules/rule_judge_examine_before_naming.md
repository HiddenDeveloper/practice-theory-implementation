---
id: rule_judge_examine_before_naming
name: List recent first; then direct reads; always record outcome
---
After judgement-oriented reads, close with a recorded outcome before the enactment ends. If the evidence supports a concern, invoke emit_friction with the grounded content and observation_data. If no Friction is warranted, leave an explicit no-finding outcome in the enactment before closing, naming the inspected target enactment id, the read/list step ids or returned basis used for the judgement, and the reason no Friction was emitted. Ending after list_recent_enactments, read_enactment_steps, read_bundle, or other inspection without either emitted Friction or an explicit no-finding outcome is rule neglect: a later practitioner cannot tell whether the Judge found no concern or simply stopped after reading.

For a dispatched or otherwise known target, list_recent_enactments is the first judgement-oriented invocation: do it before read_enactment_steps, read_bundle, or any other direct target inspection, even when the target id is already supplied.

The ordering boundary is temporal in the target's own step sequence: if read_enactment_steps, read_bundle, or another direct target inspection appears before list_recent_enactments, a later discovery-window read does not satisfy this rule merely because all required reads eventually occurred.

Before naming Friction, first invoke list_recent_enactments (filtered to the target's bundle when known) so the discovery window is recorded before any direct read of steps or bundle; then examine the target on the trail and ground every claim in something the trail records -- never name Friction without trail evidence.

Situate the target first: invoke list_recent_enactments (filtered to the target's bundle when known) so the discovery window is recorded; a direct read of a dispatched or otherwise known target is not a substitute for this discovery-window read. If a dispatched target is absent from that window, preserve the list-versus-direct mismatch in observation_data rather than treating absence as proof about its steps. Do not emit a trail_discoverability_gap or recent_discovery_gap solely because a known dispatched target is absent from this bounded opened_at listing when direct read_enactment_steps and read_bundle both resolve the target and the listing surface already exposes its window boundary; in that case, record the mismatch as context for the judgement instead of making it the judgement. This exception only prevents the bounded omission itself from becoming an automatic judgement. If the Judge concludes that the mismatch is nevertheless a discoverability concern because the listing behavior is misleading, undocumented by the returned material result, repeated at larger limits, or otherwise relevant to future practitioners, then emit that Friction with observation_data showing the direct read, bundle resolution, and listing attempts; if no Friction is warranted, leave a concrete no-finding outcome explaining why the grounded mismatch is not Friction. Do not close silently after grounding such a mismatch.

Then read the target's steps and its bundle. Keep the same enactment id as the comparison subject across the discovery window, read_enactment_steps, and read_bundle basis: do not compare steps read from one enactment against the available affordances for another enactment's bundle. If a read_enactment_steps call returns or was invoked for a different enactment id than the target being judged, resolve that identity mismatch through a corrected read before judging, or name a trail_identity_gap with observation_data showing both ids and the bundle basis; do not issue behavioural Friction or no-finding from a cross-target comparison.

Reread a target's steps only when the second read has a distinct grounding purpose, such as checking a different enactment id, confirming changed state after an intervening action, or comparing a newly surfaced target. Do not invoke read_enactment_steps twice with the same material and identical arguments as a reflex before judgement. If an identical reread is necessary, make the reason visible in the subsequent outcome or observation_data; otherwise proceed from the first returned step sequence to the bundle comparison, no-finding outcome, or emitted Friction.

read_bundle resolves any current practice bundle by id -- somatic and engagement bundles (e.g. continuous_self) included -- even though list_practices shows only what this seat can switch into. So complete the available-versus-used comparison; do not skip it, or withhold narrow_engagement, on the false belief that a somatic or engagement bundle is unreadable from the autonomic seat. The only genuinely unreadable case is a bundle id that no longer exists -- renamed or removed (e.g. a stale user_focused_engagement, a deleted quick_glance); for that, name a missing_bundle / stale-reference Friction, not a behavioural one.

If a Friction quotes or depends on substrate text (a rule, understanding, or description), make that text visible before relying on it: prefer a recorded read that exposes the prose; if the only available read exposes ids but not prose, include the relied-on substrate id and a short excerpt or exact phrase in the emitted Friction's observation_data so the trail carries the evidence source boundary. If the text cannot be retrieved or excerpted, name the evidence/readability gap rather than presenting unseen text as trail-grounded.

For a session-scope holder such as continuous_self: a thin or empty direct step list is not abandonment. Check whether child practice enactments are visible (via parent_enactment_id) and read or account for them before judging engagement breadth; if child visibility cannot be grounded, name that gap rather than emitting narrow_engagement from the holder's direct steps alone.

For a no-finding outcome, make the closure concrete rather than implicit: name the inspected target enactment id, the read/list step ids or returned basis used for the judgement, and the reason no Friction was emitted. Ending immediately after read_enactment_steps, read_bundle, or list_recent_enactments leaves the judgement outcome unrecorded even when the reads themselves succeeded.
