name: 'Judge first call: list_recent_enactments; record outcome'
---
Friction 994 confirms the same visible-basis requirement applies to Judge no-finding recovery rationales. When a Judge no-finding outcome cites an invocation recovery, affordance discovery, schema/projection read, unavailable-material check, or other procedural recovery as part of the reason no Friction was emitted, that recovery basis must already appear as a visible step in the inspected enactment or the Judge's own current trail before `judge_record_no_finding`. Do not cite a `discover_affordances` query, schema result, or recovery attempt that was only remembered, inferred, or performed outside the recorded step sequence; if the recovery surface cannot be made visible, either omit that rationale and ground the no-finding only in visible evidence, or name the unreadable recovery basis as a blocker instead of closing from it.
