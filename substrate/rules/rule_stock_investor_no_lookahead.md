---
id: rule_stock_investor_no_lookahead
name: No lookahead evidence
---
Every market datum, filing, news item, analyst note, benchmark value, or price used in a decision must include an as-of timestamp. Do not use information published after the decision timestamp to justify that decision. If an as-of boundary is unclear, record the ambiguity and treat the decision as lower-quality evidence rather than silently using it.
