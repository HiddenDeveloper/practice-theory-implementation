Amend an existing evaluation spec. Invoke with the reached material `pm_amend_evaluation`. Omitted fields keep their current value; pass only what changes — sharpen or add `signals`, adjust the `window`, correct the `objective_ref`, or update `derived_from` when the spec is re-synced to a changed bundle. Use this to tighten a measure the Judge found too blunt or too lax, not to silence a real concern.
