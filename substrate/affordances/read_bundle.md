Return a bundle's structure as data: its mode and the pool ids it selects. This exposes ids, not the prose of the selected rules, understanding, teleo-affective elements, affordances, or materials. Invoke this affordance with the reached material `judge_read_bundle`; names such as `practice_catalog` are stale aliases and are not valid material names. When a Judge Friction quotes or depends on substrate text, this read is not by itself a visible text basis; pair the observation with a reachable prose read when available, or include the relied-on substrate id plus a short excerpt or exact phrase in observation_data. For rule_neglect or similar judgements that rely on a selected rule, make the relied-on rule id and the rule phrase/excerpt visible in observation_data rather than treating the id-only bundle read as sufficient textual evidence.
