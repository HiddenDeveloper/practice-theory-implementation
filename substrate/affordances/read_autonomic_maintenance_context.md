Read recent Smoother enactments as maintenance context: which Friction each Smoother tended, why the enactment existed, what actions it took, whether its closure basis is visible, and which substrate ids it changed. Use this to distinguish broken behavior, active repair, completed repair, stale historical repair targets, and newly proposed substrate changes.
