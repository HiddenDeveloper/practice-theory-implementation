Read the current Friction-kind vocabulary with counts, most common first. Consult it before renaming a Friction so a provisional name can be condensed toward a kind that already carries gravity, rather than minting yet another one-off. The kinds with high counts are the condensed canonical forms; the long tail of single-occurrence kinds are provisional names awaiting condensation.
