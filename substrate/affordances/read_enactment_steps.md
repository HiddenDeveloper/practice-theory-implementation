Return the full sequence of steps recorded against a single enactment, with affordances, materials, arguments, and results. Invoke this affordance with the reached material `judge_read_enactment_steps`; the bundle id `judge` is not a material name, and names such as `trail` or `practice_trail` are stale aliases and are not valid material names.
