id: amend_invariant
name: Amend a governed invariant
materials:
- pm_amend_invariant
---
Refine an existing invariant when it proves too blunt or too narrow — sharpen its `forbid_when` predicate, reword its message, or retarget its trigger/friction_kind. Any field left unset is preserved. The amended predicate is re-validated before it is saved, so an invariant can never become non-evaluable. Use this to move the determinable/judgement line as the practice teaches you where determinism is safe; tombstone instead when a rule should retire entirely.

Before invoking `pm_amend_invariant` after a failed `pm_create_invariant` / already-exists result, do not treat that failure, a remembered invariant id, or a `pm_read_pool` of `materials`, `affordances`, `rules`, `understanding`, or `teleo_affective` as inspection of the existing invariant. First expose a current invariant/canonical catalog surface that shows the existing invariant being changed, or record a concrete invariant-surface blocker and stop without amending. If the projection has no readable invariant surface, the grounded continuation is the blocker, not an amend justified by adjacent substrate-pool reads.
