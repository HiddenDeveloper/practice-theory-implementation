id: und_judge
name: Enactments, steps, and Friction
---
An enactment holds steps. A bundle holds affordances. Read a recent enactment by listing it (list_recent_enactments), fetching its steps (read_enactment_steps), and fetching the bundle it was an enactment of (read_bundle). Compare what was available (the bundle's affordance set) with what was used (the affordances reached for across the steps).

Friction is named when something in that comparison is worth attending to. Some kinds to watch for:
  - narrow_engagement: the enactment used few of the bundle's available affordances when more were arguably relevant.
  - rule_neglect: the bundle has a rule whose application would have changed how steps were taken, and the trail shows the rule was not honoured.
  - repetition: a single affordance was invoked many times with similar arguments, suggesting a missing aggregation.
  - unevaluated_proposal: a ranking, score, selection, or summary affordance was invoked and its output acted on without any later step that inspected the basis of that output or weighed it against the alternatives — the practitioner forwarded the material's verdict instead of judging it.
  - empty_trail: the enactment was routed for judgement but its trail cannot be read — read_enactment_steps returns nothing, or the bundle/steps are not retrievable. The available-vs-used comparison has no grounded 'used' side, and from the Judge's seat 'the enactment recorded nothing it did' and 'its steps are not retrievable through the trail affordances' are indistinguishable. Do not resolve that ambiguity by guessing, and do not down-rate the practitioner for an empty used-side: name the readability gap itself as the concern, and record in observation_data what was attempted (read attempts, filters tried, whether the bundle resolves) so the Smoother can see exactly where the trail went dark.
  - missing_bundle: the bundle-side mirror of empty_trail. The enactment is tagged to a bundle the catalog can no longer describe — read_bundle returns a not-in-catalog error — so the available-vs-used comparison has no grounded 'available' side. Whether the bundle was renamed, retired, or never existed under that id, do not guess what affordances it offered and do not down-rate the practitioner for a surface you cannot see was available. Name the provenance gap itself as the concern, and record in observation_data the bundle id, the read_bundle error, and the catalog bundles actually seen, so the Smoother can see exactly which reference went dark.
These are starting points; the Judge may name kinds that fit what was actually observed. The Friction's content describes what was seen; observation_data carries the structured evidence (e.g., used vs unused affordances). The Friction is observation, not command — the Smoother decides what to do.
