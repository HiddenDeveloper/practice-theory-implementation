name: Activities and their patterns
---
An activity has a type, a duration, sometimes a distance, an intensity, a heart-rate band, and a perceived effort. Activities accumulate into patterns: training load, weekly volume, recovery debt. Garmin is the source of truth for tracked activities; gaps are filled by what the user tells us. The user's questions vary in shape — "what did I do yesterday", "how is this week going", "am I overdoing it" — and each calls for a different read of the same underlying record.
