name: Activities and their patterns
---
An activity has a type, a duration, sometimes a distance, an intensity, a heart-rate band, and a perceived effort. Activities accumulate into patterns: training load, weekly volume, recovery debt. Garmin Connect is the source of truth for tracked activities in this practice: activity lists, activity detail, daily wellness, and aggregate period stats should read from Garmin-native records and cite that source. Mock Garmin data is only a verification or demo surface when explicitly selected; it must not be mistaken for a device reading. Gaps are filled only by what the user tells us, and those gaps should be named. The user's questions vary in shape - "what did I do yesterday", "how is this week going", "am I overdoing it" - and each calls for a different read of the same Garmin-grounded record.

The activity list is the activity record. Daily wellness and single-activity detail are adjacent context, not substitutes for that record. When the work is to describe what was done, recent rhythm, weekly pattern, completed-activity mix, gaps, streaks, cadence, or training rhythm, the practitioner should first bring the recent Garmin activity list into view through `recent_activity` / `garmin_list_activities`, then use detail or daily summary only to deepen a selected item or explain recovery context. If the pass starts from detail, daily summary, or a stale Garmin alias and the answer would broaden beyond that isolated record, the practitioner is still missing the activity record until `garmin_list_activities` is read or an explicit list/auth/data/material gap is named.

Friction 763 confirms the remaining miss is the opening move itself: adjacent Garmin detail, daily-summary surfaces, and stale material-name attempts are being treated as workable substitutes for the recent activity record. For `activities_management`, a broad or ambiguous activity/rhythm pass is not grounded by `garmin_get_activity`, `garmin_get_daily_summary`, `garmin_get_activity_detail`, `garmin_daily_summary`, route/GPS/IWT/visualization reads, or recovery from those stale names. Until `recent_activity` reaches `garmin_list_activities`, those adjacent surfaces are only isolated context; the next move must be the list read or a concrete list/auth/data/material blocker, and any answer must stay inside the isolated supplied id/date or bounded daily snapshot.
