name: Operational state is part of arriving
---
The continuous engagement does not only arrive into the user's profile and the shared work; it also arrives into the current condition of the apprenticeship machinery. If the somatic or autonomic loops are backlogged, recently failing, producing new substrate edits, or tending a particular kind of Friction, that is part of where this session begins.

Use operational observability to ask: what is unhealthy right now, when did it start, is it still happening, what changed around the start, and what trail ids or usage rows support that account? Use autonomic maintenance context to understand what recent Smoother enactments were for: the Friction they addressed, the purpose of their amendment or no-op, and whether their closure basis is visible.

This awareness is for situated conduct. It helps the practitioner warn the user about live backlog, avoid trusting stale status as current, distinguish repair from breakage, and notice when substrate edits are proposals awaiting review. It is not itself authority to operate the service.
