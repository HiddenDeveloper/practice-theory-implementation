name: Authoring the substrate well
---
This is the shared expertise of authoring substrate — the wisdom that travels with the authoring affordances so that whoever holds them authors *well*, not merely *much*. It is pooled into both Practice Management (somatic, the human's door to deliberate authoring) and the Smoother (autonomic, the loop's door to reactive, friction-driven authoring). The same capability, the same know-how, two practices that differ only in whether a human may be in the loop.

The substrate has six pools — teleo_affective, understanding, rules, affordances, materials, and evaluations — plus a catalog of bundles. Pool entries have unique ids within their pool. Affordances reference materials by name; bundles select pool ids; an evaluation spec is selected by a bundle's `evaluation_ids`. Amendments are last-write-wins and additive; nothing is soft-deleted. Author the smallest change that does the job, and prefer adding or sharpening a pooled element over rewriting a working one.

Author data, never code, wherever the substrate lets you. An evaluation spec is data: it declares generic signal *kinds* (affordance_coverage, outcome_presence, shape_repetition, recurring_summary_marker) parameterised for one practice, plus an `objective_ref` and a prose body. It is run by the one generic engine; you do not write the engine. A good spec is *traceable to the practice's teleo-affective objective* — its `objective_ref` names one of the practice bundle's teleo-affective ids, so the evaluator demonstrably measures what the practice is *for* rather than something incidental. A spec that names no objective, or one that is not the bundle's, is a vacuous evaluator and is itself a friction. Where a practice is genuinely not yet measurable, say so honestly in the spec body rather than authoring a hollow measure. Authoring a spec does not activate it — wire its id into the bundle's `evaluation_ids` so the engine and routing pick it up.

Verify what you author where it is actually projected. An affordance, material, or evaluation authored into another bundle is not thereby projected into the authoring practice; it cannot be exercised — and so cannot be confirmed — by invoking it in place. Such a call fails with 'no affordance … in this practice', and that failure is not evidence the authoring was wrong, only that the check was aimed at the wrong projection: the thing lives on the bundle it was wired into, not on the one you are authoring from. To confirm a newly authored capability functions, exercise it on the bundle that carries it. Do not close an authoring enactment treating the change as confirmed when the only check could never have been projected here.
