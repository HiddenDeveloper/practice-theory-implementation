Friction 725 confirms the Practice Management quality concern remains genuine after another evaluated window: five of eight recent Practice Management enactments still reached reload, documentation-check, authoring, amendment, or bundle-wiring surfaces without a visible same-enactment `pm_read_pool`, while a nearby control case showed the pool-read pattern was reachable. For future Practice Management passes, treat the substrate pool read as the first visible move of any substrate-authoring path. Before reload, documentation-impact checking, `pm_create_*`, `pm_amend_*`, invariant/evaluation work, or `pm_amend_bundle`, stop and name the exact pool whose ids or current content will be relied on; if that pool has not already been exposed in the current enactment, invoke `read_pool` / `pm_read_pool` immediately and only then continue. If the pass cannot name or reach that pool surface, record a concrete substrate-surface blocker and do not proceed from the bundle description, remembered ids, prior trail, or reload/documentation context alone.

Friction 736 confirms the same gate still fails across another evaluated window, including five of eight recent Practice Management enactments that reached reload, documentation-impact, authoring, or bundle-amendment surfaces without `pm_read_pool`. Therefore the gate is not merely descriptive context: it is the first work product of substrate stewardship. At the start of any Practice Management path that may reload, check documentation impact, create, amend, author or amend invariants/evaluations, or wire a bundle, produce a visible pool-grounding row before the affected work: name the exact pool (`teleo_affective`, `understanding`, `rules`, `affordances`, or `materials`) and read it with `pm_read_pool`. If more than one pool supplies ids or content for the intended change, read each relied-on pool before the corresponding write. A bundle description, remembered id, prior Smoother friction content, or reload result is never enough to substitute for this row; when the row cannot be produced, the correct Practice Management outcome is a concrete substrate-surface blocker and no stewardship write.
