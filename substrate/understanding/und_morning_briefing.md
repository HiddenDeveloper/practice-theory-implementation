Friction 817 confirms the `reads_morning_sources_before_assembling` quality concern persists across another evaluated window: all three inspected `morning_briefing` passes lacked `gmail_user_search_threads`, and two also lacked `read_morning_briefing_sites`, even though the bundle already says the briefing starts from unread email and the configured usual-site list. For future `morning_briefing` passes, the first visible work product is the recurring source baseline, in order: `read_user_email` reaching `gmail_user_search_threads`, then `read_morning_site_list` reaching `read_morning_briefing_sites`. If either row is absent, the pass is interrupted until the exact missing row is read or a concrete Gmail/site-list auth, access, configuration, data, or material blocker is recorded. Do not proceed from site checks, browser checks, invalid URL handling, live market snapshots, source interpretation, assembly prose, or final answers while either recurring source row is absent and unblocked; those surfaces may only deepen the briefing after the baseline row or blocker is visible.

Friction 860 confirms the remaining failure is not lack of source prose but lack of a visible opening ledger: recent `morning_briefing` passes still skipped unread email in all three evaluated enactments and skipped the configured site-list read in two. Therefore the morning briefing starts with exactly two current-enactment baseline rows before any downstream briefing work: `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail, then `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` for the configured recurring sites. If a pass reaches `capture_morning_site_check`, `morning_briefing_browser_site_check`, live market or browser checks, source interpretation, assembly, final answer, or any briefing summary before both rows are present, the briefing is unfinished; the immediate next move is the missing baseline row or a concrete blocker for that exact row. A successful site-list row does not substitute for unread email, and a browser/site check does not substitute for the configured site-list row.

Friction 875 confirms the same `reads_morning_sources_before_assembling` failure remains genuine even when one pass did read the configured site list: across the evaluated three-enactment window, no pass read unread email through `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads`, and two passes also skipped `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` before moving into browser or briefing activity. Treat unread email as the first non-skippable baseline row, not as one source among several. If the site-list row appears first, the pass is still interrupted until the unread-email row or a concrete Gmail auth/access/data/material blocker is visible; if browser/site checks, live market snapshots, source interpretation, assembly, final answer, or summary activity appear before either baseline row, the only permitted continuation is the exact missing baseline read or its concrete blocker. Do not count configured-site reads, browser checks, invalid-URL handling, remembered source context, or periodic-briefing intent as satisfying the unread-email baseline.

Friction 916 confirms the source-coverage failure remains a practice-quality concern after the bundle description already names both recurring reads: in the evaluated window, all three `morning_briefing` passes still missed unread-email grounding, and two also missed the configured site-list read. For future `morning_briefing` passes, treat any downstream briefing surface before the two-row baseline as an interruption, not progress: the immediate next visible move must be `read_user_email` / `gmail_user_search_threads` for unread mail if absent, then `read_morning_site_list` / `read_morning_briefing_sites` if absent, or a concrete blocker for the exact missing row. Do not continue from a successful site-list read, browser/site check, invalid URL handling, live market snapshot, source interpretation, briefing assembly, summary, final answer, or closure while unread email remains unread and unblocked; unread email is the first baseline row, and the site-list row is the second baseline row.
