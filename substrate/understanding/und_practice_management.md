name: The substrate's shape
---
The substrate has five pools — teleo_affective, understanding, rules, affordances, materials — plus a catalog of bundles. Pool entries have unique ids within their pool. Affordances reference materials by name. Bundles select pool ids. Amendments are last-write-wins; nothing is soft-deleted. The captured surface of a material can be authored, and a dynamic implementation can be registered into the function registry.

When this substrate is tended autonomically, no engagement layer is projected, and so there is no canonical-self or AI-role record to consult for grounding — that self-dossier belongs to somatic mode, where a practice inherits it from the engagement. The autonomic practitioner orients by reading the substrate itself (read_pool across the five pools and the bundle catalog), not by consulting a self. There is nothing to ground in here beyond what the pools hold; do not reach for an about_ai_role / consult_canonical_self read in this bundle — it is not afforded, and the expectation of one is the thing to set down.

The same boundary governs bundle reads. `read_bundle` / `judge_read_bundle` is the Judge's comparison surface for available-versus-used judgement, not a Practice Management catalog read to borrow from inside this bundle. When tending bundles from Practice Management, use only the Practice Management affordances projected here: read the pools and the projected affordance/material descriptions, then author or amend through the PM surfaces. If the intended bundle preview or confirmation cannot be grounded through the PM surface, record that surface gap or enact the bundle that owns the affordance; do not invoke a Judge-only affordance from Practice Management.

The same boundary governs how you verify what you author here. An affordance or material authored into another bundle is not thereby projected into the authoring practice; it cannot be exercised — and so cannot be verified — by invoking it in place. Such a call fails with 'no affordance … in practice practice_management', and that failure is not evidence the authoring was wrong, only that the verification was aimed at the wrong projection: the affordance lives on the bundle it was wired into, not on this one. To confirm a newly-authored affordance actually functions, enact the bundle that carries it and exercise it there. Do not close an authoring enactment treating the change as confirmed when the only check was an in-place invocation that could never have been projected here.
