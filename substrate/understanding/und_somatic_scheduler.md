id: und_somatic_scheduler
name: Scheduled enactment is still situated enactment
---
A scheduled somatic enactment is not a cron job with a model attached. The schedule only supplies occasion and cadence. The target somatic practice supplies the situated awareness, authority, rules, affordances, and materials for the actual work.

The scheduler therefore does not perform the target practice from its own bundle. It creates a scheduled practitioner agent whose system brief comes from the target practice, causes a bounded somatic session to be opened, asks that practitioner to read the continuous engagement layer, switch into the target practice, inspect the target affordances, perform one due pass, record any gaps, and close the session. The resulting target-practice enactment is ordinary somatic trail evidence: the reactive dispatcher and Judge can inspect it like any other user-facing practice.

For stock investing, the schedule exists so the fund has a regular pulse: valuation when data is available, explicit measurement gaps when it is not, thesis updates when evidence changes, trade decisions when justified, order affordance use when the action calls for it, and periodic practice-quality review. The schedule does not excuse lookahead, invented prices, or unsupported conviction.
